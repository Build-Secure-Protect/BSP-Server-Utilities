Staff panel: refined main screen

The picks from set M: look A (cards), hotbar icons, a sheet from the bottom, five tabs (D273 to D276). Four frames: the players screen, the sheet as a Moderator sees it, the sheet pulled up to full height, and the More tab. Three points (R1 to R3) to confirm before any code. Made-up names and numbers.

1. Players (R1: what a row shows)
2. A player, seen by a Moderator (R2)
3. The sheet pulled up (R3)
4. The More tab

To confirm

  1. R1. A row in the list. Ten small hearts for health, and under them the armour and hunger as an icon with a number ("▣ 12 ◆ 7"), because ten of each would not fit beside a name on a phone. A player at 3 hearts or less gets a red LOW mark. The full rows of icons are in the sheet.
  2. R2. Actions a rank cannot use. They stay in place, dimmed with the rank that may use them written under them ("Admin"), so every staff member sees the same layout. Tapping one says who can do it. The alternative is to hide them, so a Moderator sees only four keys.
  3. R3. Pulling the sheet up. Dragging the sheet to the top turns it into the whole player page, in this order: stats and effects, the inventory (as in the game: armour and offhand beside the 27 slots and the hotbar, then the ender chest, Curios and backpacks further down), the notes, then this player's recent Action Log lines. Inventory edits (the + and − keys) come once InventorySync's api is built (D257).

The More tab (frame 4) lists what does not fit in the bottom tabs: Broadcast, Bans, Action Log, Market, Lottery, Item icons and Settings. Market and Lottery are marked "soon" until BSP-Core's api is built (SU-BC1).
