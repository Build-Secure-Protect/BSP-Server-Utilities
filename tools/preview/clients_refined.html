To confirm

  1. R1 blocking something a player online has: the entry is added, the players online with it get a red flag "blocked since they joined" and are refused at their next join, and nobody is kicked by the block itself (default); or everyone online with it is kicked at once with the refusal message.
  2. R2 "Refused today": since midnight on the server's clock, newest first, up to 20 (default); or the last 24 hours, whatever the time of day.
  3. R3 TRUST MY SETUP while players are online: everyone online is judged again against the new setup at once, so their flags change, and nobody is kicked (default); or only players who join afterwards are judged against it.
