The lines in the game (the same for every look)

The refusal (D442) on the action bar; the over-limit warning when entering one's own room (D440); the notice when a room loses its last machine; giving back.

Refused: a new room at the limit (action bar)
You own 5 of 5 Compact Machine rooms. Give one back in /su rooms to make another.
Entering one of your rooms while over the limit (chat)
You own 7 Compact Machine rooms; the limit is 5, so you are 2 over.Give back 2 in /su rooms to come down to the limit. You can keep using them, but you cannot make another until you are under it.
A room loses its last machine (chat; at once, or at the next login)
The machine of your Compact Machine room on Frostwarf (tiny) was destroyed (burnt).It no longer counts against your limit. Its contents are cleared in 7 days unless a machine bound to it is placed again.
Giving back (GIVE BACK, then SURE? within three seconds)
You gave back your room "Farm" on Frostwarf. It no longer counts; it and everything in it are cleared in 7 days unless you place its machine again.

To choose

  1. Z1 the player's page: look A, a list with one line per room and its details below (default); look B, tiles drawn to each room's size; look C, rooms grouped under each server.
  2. Z2 the admins' tab: look A, the owners table with the picked owner's rooms beside it (default); look B, usage bars plus the list of rooms in grace; look C, server chips above a wider owners table.
  3. Z3 rooms on other servers: listed in full on the player's page (size, name, machine placed or not, state), which means every server shares its room list, not only its counts (default); or only a count per other server.
  4. Z4 giving back or freeing a room on another server: only on that room's own server, the line saying where (default); or from any server, the request sent to the room's server, which applies the same checks.
  5. Z5 the over-limit warning (D440): in chat each time the owner enters one of their rooms while over, at most once a minute (default); or on the action bar; or once per login.
  6. Z6 the notice when a room loses its last machine: told in chat at once if the owner is online on that server, otherwise at their next login on any server (default); or no notice, only the page.
  7. Z7 where the machine stands: the player sees their own machines' coordinates (default); or only "placed" or "not placed".
  8. Z8 a machine item still bound to a room that has been cleared (copies in chests cannot be found): placed as a fresh, unbound machine, so it makes a new room under the limit as any other (default); or refused with a line, the item kept.
