Tutorial builder: three options for each screen (sets AV to AZ)

The owner's answers D719 to D736 drawn as screens: several tutorials (one offered on first join, the others started by walking into an area), popups that freeze the player or fade while they walk, a text editor that formats mid-line, a tutorial_images/ folder, small popups dragged anywhere, an OPEN LINK button. Each frame is the 480 × 270 GUI-pixel screen (1920 × 1080 at GUI scale 4); the large ones are drawn 1.5 times bigger. The tutorials, places and numbers are samples; the world is a drawn stand-in. Admin screens are violet, what players see is teal and gold, as in the rest of the mod.

Set AV: the tree builder

Opened from OPEN BUILDER on the TUTORIAL tab (D719). Every option shows the same sample: "Welcome to BSP", offered on first join, forking at Spawn Hall into a full tour and a quick start. A link's line says how the player gets from one point to the next: a walked path (gold), none (the next popup opens at once), or a move to another server (violet).

A. The advancements screen: a canvas of nodes, a side drawer for the one picked

Tabs across the top are the tutorials (★ is the first-join one). Nodes are dragged on a dotted canvas you pan and zoom; dragging from one node to another links them. Clicking a node fills the drawer: its popup, its place, its links, each with DRAW PATH or REDRAW. A red dot marks something missing (here, a link with no path from a point that has a place).

B. A flow from top to bottom, with the tutorials and their start in a left column

Each point is a card with its popup's kind; each link carries a badge (PATH 42 BLOCKS, NO PATH, TELEPORT). A fork splits into columns named after the buttons that choose them. Picking a link shows DRAW PATH under it. The left column holds every tutorial with how it starts, and the picked one's start settings.

C. An outline on the left, the points on a plan of the world on the right

The tree as an indented list (easy to read with many points), next to a top-down plan of where the points are, one server and world at a time, with the walked paths in gold. The plan is drawn from the points and paths only, with no terrain. A point picked in either is picked in both.

Set AW: the popup editor

Full screen, opened by EDIT POPUP on a node: the layout grid on the left and the preview on the right (brief). The grid is 12 × 8 for a full-screen popup and 6 × 4 for a small one (D724); a box covers a rectangle of cells you drag out. Text boxes use a new editor that formats inside a line (D725); images come from tutorial_images/ or a web address (D726).

A. Halves: grid with the box's editor under it, preview with the buttons under it

Everything on one page. The popup's own settings (full or small, freezes or keeps walking, background) run across the top. Selecting text in the editor and pressing B, I, U, S, a colour or a size changes only that part, as in a word processor.

B. Three columns: settings and buttons, the grid, the preview

The grid is the biggest thing on the page. A box's settings float over the grid next to it (here an image box: a file from the folder or a web address, and its fit). A button's job is picked from a list, with a branch or a web address where the job needs one (D728).

C. Tabs on the left, a large preview on the right

LAYOUT, TEXT, IMAGE, BUTTONS and POPUP tabs share the left side; the preview is the largest of the three options. The TEXT tab is open here, with the editor at full width.
A small popup's POPUP tab (D723, D727): it keeps the player walking and fades after 8 seconds. The preview becomes the whole screen, where the popup is dragged anywhere and resized by its corner. Guides snap it to the centre and the edges. Its place is kept as a share of the screen, so it lands in the same spot at every window size.

Set AX: what the player sees

Three looks, each shown three ways: a full-screen popup, the first-join offer straight after the rules (YES, NO THANKS, LATER; D722), and a small popup that does not freeze the player and fades as they walk (D723).

A. Holo card: the mod's own panels

A framed card with the tutorial's name, the step on this branch and a thin progress line. END TUTORIAL sits apart on the left, the moving-on keys on the right.
The offer, centred, after the rules popup closes.
A popup that keeps the player walking: no keys, a bar that empties, then it fades.

B. Guide: gold, with the stops as dots

A gold band on top with a dot per stop on this branch (the one lit is where you are). The buttons are gold outlines; the main one is filled.
The offer as a gold card with the tutorial's name large.
A walking popup as a toast with a gold edge that shortens with the time left.

C. Floating: no frame, the content over the darkened game

The boxes float on the darkened game with no outer frame, so the admin's layout is the whole look. Large keys centred at the bottom.
The offer in the same floating style.
A walking popup as a speech bubble with how far the next stop is.

Set AY: drawing a path

DRAW PATH on a link teleports the admin to its first point. START PATH records a point every 3 blocks walked or flown (the yellow line); the next point is a transparent sphere of its radius; inside it, FINISH PATH (D731). While walking the mouse steers the view, so the mode's actions are keys shown on screen.

A. A strip across the top, the finish as a card

The strip says what is being drawn, how many points and blocks so far, and how far is left; the keys are under it. The admin has turned round to look back at the line from The Bank; The Market's sphere is ahead on the right.
Inside the sphere: the edge of the screen glows and the card asks to finish. KEEP WALKING closes it and goes on recording.

B. A checklist card on the left, an arrow to the target

The steps ticked off as they happen, the numbers and the keys in one card; an arrow above the hotbar points at the sphere with the distance.
Inside the sphere, the card's last step lights up and FINISH PATH appears beside the crosshair.

C. Out of the way: the action bar, a compass, chat links

Nothing covers the view: a compass strip with the target, one line above the hotbar, and clickable chat lines to undo or cancel (opening chat frees the mouse).
Inside the sphere, a title and a chat line with FINISH PATH and KEEP WALKING.

Set AZ: the player's golden path and the sign at the end

Only the player on the tutorial sees it. It floats about a block above the walked line, is faint through walls and fades with distance (D732). Each option has a close-up of its sign in the corner.

A. Dashes, and a sign on a post

Short gold dashes, like a dotted line on a map. At the end a translucent sign on a thin post names the next stop. No other HUD.

B. Glowing dots that ripple forward, and a floating panel

Round gold dots a block apart; every fourth is brighter and the bright ones move forward, showing the way. The sign is a floating panel in the holo look. A small line at the top says the next stop and how far it is.

C. Chevrons, a banner and a ring on the ground

Gold chevrons pointing the way. At the end, a banner over a ring on the ground the size of the trigger radius, so the player can see where the popup opens. A compass mark at the top.
