The Display Panel, refined

Your picks from set B drawn together for a last look before SU-5 to SU-8 are built: the slim-bezel block (D41) at full brightness (D42), and the settings screen with the big clickable preview and the inspector (D43), sizes that scale with the panel (D44), the 16 colours and a #RRGGBB box (D45), 16 lines of 80 characters (D46), dark or clear backgrounds (D47), SAVE and CLOSE (D48) and the image pane (D49). The staff-online and action-log faces are set C.

1. The block in the world

Left to right: a single block, a 4 x 2 panel with text, and a 2 x 2 panel with a clear background, where the wall shows through behind the text. The bezel runs round the outside of each joined panel only; the teal light sits in the bottom-right corner. The block is 3 pixels deep against the wall it is placed on, like BSP-Core's Score Screen, and joins with panels of the same facing on the same wall into the largest full rectangle up to 8 x 6.

2. The settings screen (right-click the panel; admins only)

Text mode

The face fills the top: what players will see, redrawn as you type. Click a line on it to pick it (outlined in violet). The inspector under it edits the picked line: its text with the count against 80, the size (S, M, L, XL) with − and +, the colour (a click on the swatch opens the 16 Minecraft colours and a # box, drawn open here), the alignment and bold. + ADD LINE adds one under the picked line (4 / 16 counts them); UP and DOWN move it; DELETE LINE asks SURE?. The modes run down the left with the background under them. Nothing reaches the panel in the world until SAVE; CLOSE (or Escape) leaves it as it was.
