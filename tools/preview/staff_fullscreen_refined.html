The staff screen, almost full screen: refined (look B)

Look BB B, with D754 to D757 as answered. The screen is the game window less 8 GUI px on each side. A list of tabs runs down the left under group headings, and the page takes the rest. Five frames: the LOG tab at three window sizes, a settings page in two columns, and the setup guide inside the bigger frame. Sample data.

R1. 1920 × 1080 at GUI scale 4 (480 × 270 GUI): the LOG tab

The panel is 464 × 254 and the page beside the tab list 374 px wide, so the list takes the full width (as in look B). Clicking a line shows it in a "Picked line" card over the list; from 400 px of page width (R3) that card sits beside the list instead. Gold counts beside STAFF (conversations waiting on staff), CLIENTS (refused today) and DROPS (crowded chunks) are sent with the screen when it opens and every 10 seconds while it is open.

R2. 1280 × 720 at GUI scale 3 (427 × 240 GUI): the smallest common window

The panel is 411 × 224, still bigger than the current 344 × 210. Below 254 px tall the tab list packs tighter (11 px rows, 9 px headings), so all 13 tabs fit without scrolling. As in R1, a clicked line shows in a card over the list.

R3. 1920 × 1080 at GUI scale 2 (960 × 540 GUI), drawn at half size

The panel is 944 × 524 (D756: never capped). The list shows about 40 lines, and its WHAT column takes the extra width. Text stays the size it is at every window; only the amount shown grows.

R4. A settings page in two columns (D757): the AFK tab at 480 × 270

The settings boxes keep their 180 px width. On the left are AFK DETECTION, KICKING and MACROS; on the right AFK NOW fills the rest of the page, so it shows more players. When the page is narrower than two boxes (R2's size), the boxes stack and AFK NOW goes under them.

R5. The setup guide inside the bigger frame

The guide's strip runs along the top under the title: which page, a dot per page, and the feature's one-line description. The tab list shows where you are (done pages in teal, the current one in violet) but cannot be clicked, as today. NEXT, SETUP LATER and TURN OFF FEATURE sit in the footer.

Built as

  • The screen's size and every tab's layout are worked out from the window when it opens and on every resize, never fixed numbers. Each tab gets its page's rectangle (left, top, width, height) to lay out in.
  • Lists show more rows and the extra width goes to their widest column; settings boxes keep 180 px and go into two columns from 380 px of page width.
  • The tab list is 74 px wide at every size: 12 px rows and 12 px headings, or 11 and 9 below 254 px tall. The open tab is violet with a bar on its left. A tab is opened by clicking it, or with the up and down arrow keys while no box is being typed in.
  • The title bar (18 px) holds the title, this server and how many are online, and a close key (×); Escape still closes.
  • Every string goes through the fit and wrap helpers, and a layout audit with Minecraft's real glyph widths runs at 427, 480, 640 and 960 GUI widths before it is called done.
