Streamer Mode: three looks for the menu (set AU)

The menu where a streamer turns Streamer Mode on, picks how OBS gets the clean picture, and chooses per mod what OBS must not show (D700 to D712). Each frame is the 480 × 270 GUI-pixel screen (1920 × 1080 at GUI scale 4), drawn 1.5 times larger. The mods, items and counts are this pack's (the research in the vault); the world behind is a drawn stand-in.

A. Two panes: mods on the left, their items on the right

Settings in one top bar. Choosing a mod on the left lists what it can hide on the right; each switch reads HIDDEN (teal) or SHOWN. The counts say how many are hidden.
The buttons: a small STREAMER key in the top-left corner of the pause screen and the title screen, like the inventory's TEAM and KITS keys. A red dot while Streamer Mode is on.

B. One list: every mod a section you open

The settings first, then each mod as a row that opens in place (JourneyMap and BSP-Teleport open here). One scrolling page, no second column.
The buttons: a full-width "Streamer Mode" button in the pack's button look, a row below the last vanilla button, on the pause screen and the title screen.

C. Overview with a live preview, then a page per mod

The first page: the switch, the output and the frame rate, a live preview of exactly what OBS gets (here the minimap is gone from it), and a tile per mod.
A mod's page, after clicking its tile: its items with their switches, and BACK.
The buttons: a small square key with an eye icon in the top-right corner (its name on hover), red-ringed while on.

The same in every look: what OBS gets while a hidden menu is open

The stream picture while the player has JourneyMap's full-screen map open: the game behind it, no minimap, and a small card where the menu would be. The player's own window shows the map as usual.

To pick

  1. The look: A, B or C. A shows the most at once; B is the simplest to read on a small window; C adds the live preview and keeps each mod's list short. Parts can be mixed (for example A with C's preview).
  2. The buttons come with each look but can be swapped: the corner key (A), the full-width button (B) or the eye key (C).
  3. Hidden at first switch-on (D707): everything that shows a place (F3, chat lines with coordinates, JourneyMap's minimap, markers and menus, BSP-Teleport's coordinates, the sentinel strip, the recall card, the alliance and Land Office screens). Shown: the player list, boss bars, titles, the action bar, every other mod.
  4. The note at the bottom says what can never be hidden: what is drawn into the world.
