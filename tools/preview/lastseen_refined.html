A grey OFFLINE chip, then the words in the small grey of today's "online · Frostwarf". When: "1 min ago (21:04)" to "59 min ago", "3 h ago (21:04)" under a day, "yesterday (22:41)" for the calendar day before, "4 d ago" to 13 days, then "18 Oct" ("18 Oct 2025" in an earlier year). Then "on BSP-Main" (the server's name, as in su status) and the world, left out for the Overworld: "nether", "end", or a modded world's name with its underscores as spaces ("twilight forest"). When the line is too wide, the grey part is cut with "…" (R1c), and the hover has all of it. The "3 h ago" moves on while the window stays open.

  1. R3 The other states (D675, D676): a player with no record shows the chip and "last seen: not recorded", with the hover of R3a. When the database did not answer, or on a server without shared storage, the head is today's one line, "offline". An online player, here or on another server, keeps "online · Frostwarf" and no database read is made.
  2. R4 The window (D682): the second line is 8 GUI px tall, and the conversation is 8 GUI px shorter only while it shows. The window's height is otherwise as built (as tall as the screen allows, up to 167). The line is read once as the window opens (D677). If the player logs on while it is open, the head stays until the window is opened again, as "online · ..." does today.
  3. R5 The server (D677 to D680): when the player is not online on any server, the server reads the player's row in bsp_su_seen together with the conversation, on the storage worker (one query). It sends how long ago (its own now minus the row's time, never below 0), the server, the world, the block, and whether it was a logout. The staff member's client turns "how long ago" into its own clock time, so a client clock that is off still says "3 h ago". The row is trusted as the mod's other shared tables are; the signed copy stays the panel's (D679). A read that fails sends "no answer", which is R3b. The window packet changes, so this is protocol 11 (D680): every server needs the new jar and players the new modpack together. Mod 0.19.0; the panel is unchanged at 0.7.0 (D681).
