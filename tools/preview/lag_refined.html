The LAG tab: refined (look C)

Look BA C with D758 to D761 as answered: a map of chunks around a centre, coloured by what you are looking for, with a side panel for the picked chunk. LOADED and PROFILER change what the map shows; LIST shows the same chunks as a table. Seven frames: the two LOADED colourings, the server menu, the list, a sample running, its results, and the outline in the world, plus the smallest and largest windows. Sample data.

R1. LOADED, coloured by entities (480 × 270)

The map opens on this server and the world you are in, centred on you (the white square). A chunk not loaded is near black. The squares are as big as the window allows: 17 × 17 chunks here, more on bigger windows, and the wheel zooms out (×2 per step, up to 4 steps) and in. Drag to move; HOME goes back to you. Clicking a square picks it: its details on the right, GO THERE (the highest safe block at its middle, D750). The legend counts what each colour means; the footer gives the totals for this world and the network.

R2. LOADED, coloured by why each chunk is loaded, with the server menu open

WHY colours each chunk by what keeps it loaded: players nearby (teal), a mod's ticket (violet), /forceload (gold), the spawn area (blue), other (grey). The server menu lists each server's worlds with how many chunks are loaded in each; other servers answer when the tab opens (D748). A server not heard from is grey and shows its last answer's age. BLOCK ENT. colours by block entities the same way as R1.

R3. LIST: the same chunks as a table, every server

LIST shows the chunks of every server and world, sortable by any column (click its heading). SHOW ON MAP opens the map at the picked chunk. TP on a row on another server goes through BSP-Teleport, and is greyed without it.

R4. PROFILER, a sample running

The server menu picks where the sample runs (D761); 10, 30 or 60 seconds (D749); RUN SAMPLE. While it runs, every editor who opens LAG sees the bar, the time left and who started it; STOP ends it early with what it has. One sample at a time on each server. The map stays coloured by the last sample until the new one is in.

R5. PROFILER, the results

The map is coloured by milliseconds per tick. The side panel ranks the picked chunk's block entities and entities (grouped by kind, with the worst one's place), and its own tick (random ticks, spawning); TP stands you beside the worst of that kind (D750). OUTLINE marks the top 10 blocks of the whole sample with a red box only you see, for five minutes (D751). WORST ANYWHERE ranks the chunks of every world on that server; clicking one moves the map there. The header line gives the tick and the share not in any chunk (D760). The results stay until the next sample or a restart (D759).

R6. The outline in the world, after OUTLINE

Red boxes round the top blocks, seen through walls, with a small label of the block and its ms per tick; they fade after five minutes, or at once with OUTLINE again. Only the editor who pressed it sees them.

R7. The smallest window (427 × 240) and the largest (960 × 540, drawn at half size)

At 427 × 240 the map shows 13 × 13 and the side panel narrows. The world key drops the server's name (the menu still shows it), and the colour keys shorten to ENT., B.E. and WHY.

At 960 × 540 the map shows 33 × 33 and the side panel lists more.

Built as

  • SU-149 (server): a snapshot of each world's loaded chunks (position, why and whose, entities by type, block entities by type), sent for the map's area or the list's page; other servers asked on demand through Redis.
  • SU-151 (server): the profiler. A mixin times every block entity's tick and every entity's tick, and each chunk's own tick, only during a sample (nothing outside one). Results are kept per server until the next sample or a restart, shared with every editor; Action Log kinds lag_sample and chunk_tp (D752).
  • SU-150 and SU-152 (client): the map, the side panel, the list, the controls and the outline renderer.
  • Editors only (D747). Every string through the fit and wrap helpers; a layout audit with real glyph widths at four sizes before it is called done.
