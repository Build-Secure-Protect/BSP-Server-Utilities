Sets AQ, AR and AS: offline players

Three looks each for the Players tab's list with offline players (set AQ), the offline player's card (set AR), and the Map tab's players window with "Show offline players" and the place where someone logged off (set AS). Already decided and drawn in all of them:

  • Offline players are greyed but readable, with the most recently seen first, 50 at a time (D653, D654).
  • The search box finds online and offline players by any part of the name (D655).
  • The card shows no stats, the place they logged off with MAP ›, and the items from the newest save. Kick and Teleport are left out; Graves, Bank, Message and Ban stay (D658, D659).
  • On the map, a click on an offline player switches to their server and world, with a grey pin until another player is chosen (D661, D662).

Pick one look per set; you may combine. All names, places and items are sample data.

A click opens a popup at the pin (name, when, the place, ITEMS, NOTES, OPEN CARD) instead of a corner card.

In every look:

  • The switch is remembered in this browser (D661), and the window's search finds offline players by any part of the name.
  • The pin stays until another player is chosen or the card is closed (D662).
  • A world whose map an Owner hid, or that BlueMap never drew, shows the card with "That world's map is not shown" and the place in words.
  • A player who logs on while chosen turns into the live player being followed.

What happens after you pick

Answer per set, for example "AQ: A, AR: A, AS: A", and combine if you like ("AR: A with look B's world-tinted tile"). The picks are drawn once more on a refined page, tools/preview/offline_refined.html, with the points to confirm. Then SU-114 starts the server side: the bsp_su_seen table and its writes.
