Staff panel: mock-up set S, the Staff tab

Three looks for the Staff tab (D265): one live staff room for every staff member on the panel, and messages to one staff member or to all staff that wait until they next open the panel. Panel only: nothing of this reaches the game. On a computer the same view fills the middle of the dashboard (the chat column stays on the right). Set R is skipped so its choices do not clash with the refined pages' R1 to R3. Made-up names.

A. The room, with messages beside it

Two halves under one switch: ROOM (the live staff room, with each person's head and rank) and MESSAGES (a red number for unread ones). Messages are cards, the unread ones with a teal edge; REPLY and GOT IT on each; + NEW MESSAGE at the top.

A1, the room
A2, messages (2 unread)

B. An inbox, like a messaging app

A list of conversations: the staff room pinned at the top, then "All staff" notices, then one row per person you have written to or heard from, each with the last line and an unread count. Tapping one opens it as a thread; under your own last message, when it was read.

B1, the list
B2, a thread with Mrgregles

C. Who is here, and what waited for you

The staff along the top as heads (ringed teal on the panel, green in game with their server, faded when away); tap a head to message them. Messages left while you were away sit in a gold box above the room until GOT IT; the room runs below. Writing a message opens a sheet with the heads to pick from, or ALL STAFF.

C1, the tab when you open the panel
C2, writing to Kip and Saffi

Choices

  1. S1. The look. A: the room and messages as two halves under one switch, messages as cards with REPLY and GOT IT. B: an inbox list with the room pinned at the top and a thread per person, like a messaging app. C: staff heads along the top, waiting messages in a gold box over the live room.
  2. S2. Read marks. The sender sees when a message was read ("Read 21:40"; for all staff, "Read by 4 of 6" with the names on a tap). Or no read marks at all.
  3. S3. How long the room and messages are kept. 30 days, as chat (D263); a message nobody has read yet is kept until it is read. Or a year. Or for ever.
  4. S4. Deleting. You may delete your own line or message within 5 minutes ("deleted" stays in its place), and Owners may delete any. Or nothing is ever deleted.
  5. S5. The Staff tab's badge. A red number for unread messages to you and room lines that name you; a plain dot for any other new room line (as Chat, D308). Or a number for every new room line too.
  6. S6. Who may write to all staff. Every rank. Or Admins and Owners only, with Moderators writing to one person at a time.
