Staff panel: mock-up set M, the main screen

Three looks for what a staff member sees first on their phone at staff.buildsecureprotect.com, after the Cloudflare code and the panel's own password and authenticator code. Each look shows the players online grouped by team (D252), with their stats (D253), and the same player opened. All three use the BSP website's colours and fonts. Names and numbers are made up.

A. Cards

A list of team cards under a strip of servers. Tap a player and a sheet rises from the bottom with their stats, tags, notes and actions. Five tabs along the bottom: Players, Chat, Map, Staff, More. Familiar from phone apps; one hand reaches everything.

A1, the players screen
A2, a player tapped

B. Board

Dense rows in the look of the in-game boards: Silkscreen headings, one line per player, more players on screen at once. Servers as a grid of tiles. A player opens as a full page with tabs (Overview, Inventory, Notes, Log) and an action bar at the bottom. Navigation in a side menu behind the top-left button.

B1, the players screen
B2, a player opened

C. Map first

The map is the home screen, with heads where players are; the teams sit in a sheet over the lower half that can be pulled up to full height. Tapping a player follows them on the map and opens a small card you can drag anywhere (the tracking card from feature 5). Same five tabs as A.

C1, the home screen
C2, a player followed

Stats: three styles

The same player (half health, hungry, under water) in three styles. Any of them can go with any look above.

Choices

  1. M1. The main screen's look. A: team cards with a sheet for a player, the easiest one-handed. B: dense board rows and full player pages, the most players on screen. C: the map as the home screen with the teams in a sheet, the quickest way to see where everyone is.
  2. M2. The stats. Hotbar icons: hearts, armour and drumsticks drawn like the game, read at a glance by anyone who plays. Bars: thin coloured bars with the numbers, more compact in a list. Numbers: "14/20" text only, the smallest.
  3. M3. How a player opens. A sheet from the bottom over the list (A2). A full page with tabs (B2). A small card you drag over the map (C2), which is used for tracking whichever is picked here.
  4. M4. Moving around. Five tabs along the bottom (A and C). A side menu behind a button (B), leaving more room for the list.
