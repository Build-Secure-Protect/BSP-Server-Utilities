Staff online and the action log: faces, admin tabs and panel panes

Set C, the last set of mock-ups. The STAFF ONLINE face (D23, D24) and the ACTION LOG face (D25, D26) as they show on a 4 x 2 Display Panel, the STAFF and LOG tabs of the admin panel, and the two panes of the panel settings that pick what each face shows. Sample names and servers; players see coordinates hidden (D26), staff see them in the LOG tab.

C1. The faces on a Display Panel (each look shows both)

Staff in a column per role, a green dot and the server for those online, dim with when they were last seen for those offline, amber AWAY; the count top right. The log as a table: how long ago, who (in their role's colour), what, which server. Plain and dense; reads from across a room on a 4 x 2.

Decisions for set C (defaults first)

  1. C1. The faces' look. Default: look A, a roster by role and a log table. Or look B, the big number and the ticker. Or look C, grouped boards.
  2. C2. Showing staff. Default: a coloured dot for each person (green online, amber away, grey offline), no player heads. Or each person's head from their skin (needs the skin to reach every player; offline players' heads may show blank at first).
  3. C3. Times. Default: how long ago ("12 min", "3 h", "2 d"). Or the server's clock time and date.
  4. C4. The admin tabs' look. Default: look A, two columns (groups beside the people; switches beside the entries). Or look B, sub-tabs with full-width tables. Or look C, role cards and a timeline.
  5. C5. Staff groups. Default: edited in the STAFF tab (picked from LuckPerms' groups, with a label and a colour each) and saved for the whole network; the config's list only fills them in the first time. Or the config only, with the tab just showing them.
  6. C6. Players reading the log (D26). Default: a command for everyone, /actionlog, opening a read-only screen with the same rows as the face (coordinates hidden), the newest 100. Or /actionlog printing the last 10 entries in chat.
  7. C7. The panel panes (C3). Default: as drawn: staff panels choose online and offline or online only, and which roles; log panels choose all servers or this one, which kinds, and 6, 8 or 12 lines. Or no choices: every panel shows everything.
  8. C8. BSP-Core's Score Screens (D22). Default: STAFF ONLINE and ACTION LOG appear as boards in BSP-Core's own board look (names and values in rows), not in the looks above. Or leave them off the Score Screens.
