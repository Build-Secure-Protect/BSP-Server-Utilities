AFK checks and macros

Mock-up set L (D207 to D219). When a player's AFK time ends (the kick time, 30 minutes here) they get a check instead of a kick: answered in 2 minutes, they are back; not answered, they are kicked. Admins and operators are never checked; other staff are never kicked. A macro's repeated input does not count as active, so macro players reach the check like anyone idle, and their machines unload after the chunk minutes. Sample data.

A card mid-screen over the game, the mouse freed; the key sits at a random place on the card each time, so a recorded click misses. The game carries on behind it. A countdown bar.
