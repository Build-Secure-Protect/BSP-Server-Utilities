2. The admins' ROOMS tab

The seventh tab of the Server utilities panel (operators and BSP-Core admins, as the other tabs). Owners on the left, most over the limit first; the picked owner's rooms on the right with their state and FREE for rooms on this server. FREE asks SURE? within three seconds, puts the room into the 7 days' grace, tells the owner (Z6) and writes a line to the action log.

To confirm

  1. R1 tile order: this server's rooms first, then other servers' rooms, then lost rooms, each group biggest first (default); or simply biggest first throughout.
  2. R2 other servers' rooms: a dimmer square on their tile, since they are reached only on their own server (default); or the same colour as this server's.
  3. R3 GIVE BACK on a room with a placed machine: shown dimmed with the line "Pick up its machine first" (default); or not shown at all.
  4. R4 the admins' owner list: sorted most over the limit first, then most rooms (default); or by name.
  5. R5 a server not heard from: after 5 minutes it is named in amber in the footer and its last figures still count (default); or its rooms stop counting until it is heard from again.
  6. R6 who sees the ROOMS tab: operators and BSP-Core admins, as the other tabs of the panel (default); or operators only.
  7. R7 the staff notice: the owner is told "Staff freed your room" without the staff member's name (default); or with the name.
