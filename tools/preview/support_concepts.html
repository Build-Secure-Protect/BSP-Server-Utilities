Three looks each for the SUPPORT BSP key in the inventory (set AG), the popup the key and /support open (set AH) and the SUPPORT tab in /su (set AI). Pick one look per set; you may combine. Sample data: the address and the sentence are made up, nothing here is live.

Answer per set, for example "AG: A, AH: B, AI: A", and combine if you like ("AH: B without the heart"). The pick is drawn once more on a refined page with two or three points to confirm, then SU-89 starts the server side.
