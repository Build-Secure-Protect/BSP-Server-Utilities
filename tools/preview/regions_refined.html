Staff panel: Worlds, regions and drawing on the map: refined

The looks picked (D764): BC A for the WORLDS tab, BD A for the editor, BE A for the Regions window, BF B for drawing. With the owner's two additions: an eye on every region to show or hide its outline (D768), and the height of a new region set in the form (D771). Nine points to confirm at the foot. The terrain is a drawn stand-in for BlueMap's; names, places and numbers are made up.

R1. The WORLDS tab

Seven servers, four across; a server that is off is greyed with when it was last heard from and lists no regions

R2. A server: its dimensions and regions

Frostwarf's Overworld chosen; the kind chips filter the table; GLOBAL SETTINGS › opens that dimension's global region in the editor

R3. The editor: a player's claim

The drawer over the table. A claim adds a CLAIM part and an audience switch over its flags: Region, Visitors, Members, Owners

R4. The editor: a mine, as a Moderator sees it

A mine adds a MINE part (block mix, reset time, where players go). A Moderator sees every value but cannot change it: the keys are dimmed and marked Admin

R5. The Map tab: the Regions window

Beside the players window, same look. Kind chips and an eye on every row; the chosen region drawn as a box of its real heights with its card

R6. Drawing: the first corner

NEW REGION turns the window into the form and asks for the first corner; the block under the pointer is shown as it moves

R7. Drawing: adjusting, refused

After the second click: handles on corners and edges, the size, and Region Guard's check, which refuses this plot for lying across plot_1

R8. Drawing: a mine, with its heights

A mine takes its heights from the two clicked blocks; Y from and Y to can be typed, or set with FULL HEIGHT or CLICKED BLOCKS. Check passed, CREATE lit

To confirm

  1. R1. Tiles. Name, online or off (and when last heard from), players, tick time, PvP world marked, each dimension, and the regions counted by kind in their colours (claims included). A click opens the server. "Find a region on any server" searches ids and names everywhere and opens the server at that region.
  2. R2. The server page. One card per dimension the server has loaded (with or without a map drawn), each with its counts and GLOBAL SETTINGS ›. The table: colour, id, name, kind, size, heights, priority, parent, and owner, price or next reset; sorted by kind then priority, any column sortable by a click on its heading. A mine's shell is not a row of its own: it is edited inside its mine. MAP › opens the Map tab on that region; EDIT › opens the drawer. + NEW REGION ON THE MAP opens the Map tab on this dimension, drawing.
  3. R3. The drawer. GENERAL (name, id, kind, priority, parent), BOUNDS (both corners, size, REDRAW ON THE MAP), PEOPLE (owners and members by name), then the part for the kind (CLAIM, PLOT, OPEN AREA, MINE or the global region's BORDER), then FLAGS: every flag Region Guard has, by its groups, the set ones first, a search, and "Inherit" showing what applies and where from. Claims, plots and open areas switch the flags between the region's own and its Visitors, Members and Owners lists. Changed values in gold until SAVE; UNDO CHANGES drops them; closing with changes asks first.
  4. R4. Moderators. The same drawer, every value shown, nothing editable, SAVE, REMOVE and RESET NOW dimmed with "Admin" (D769). Removing a claim is for Owners only (D772).
  5. R5. The Regions window. Starts folded beside the players window at its right edge, remembered in this browser apart from it (D776). Kind chips hide or show a whole kind; the eye on a row hides or shows that one region; both remembered in this browser (D768). Rows list the map shown, by kind. A click on a row or on a rectangle switches server and dimension if needed, zooms to fit, draws the region as a box of its real heights and opens its card (D777). REGIONS in the map's bar hides every outline at once.
  6. R6. The first corner. NEW REGION (Admins and Owners) asks for the kind first, then the first click. The map still pans and zooms while drawing; Esc or CANCEL stops. Clicks on other regions' rectangles set a corner instead of opening their card while drawing.
  7. R7. Adjusting. Corner handles move two sides, edge handles one; the corners in the form follow, and typing in them moves the outline. Region Guard's check runs a moment after each change (SU-RG5's dry run): green when fine, red with its reason when not, and CREATE stays dim until it passes.
  8. R8. Heights. Y from and Y to, typed or set with FULL HEIGHT (the world's bottom to top) or CLICKED BLOCKS (the two blocks clicked). Regions, plots and open areas start at full height; mines start at the clicked blocks (D771). After CREATE the window goes back to the list with the new region chosen; the same heights can be changed later in the editor.
  9. R9. Phones. WORLDS is the first line of More (D765); a server's regions as a list by dimension with the kind chips; the editor as a full-height sheet. On the map, a REGIONS key beside the players key opens a sheet with the same list; drawing is offered on a computer only.

Or say what to change.
