Tutorial builder: the refined screens (from sets AV to AZ)

The owner's picks (D744: AV A, AW A, AX A, AY A, AZ A) with the answers D719 to D743 worked in: several tutorials, a start area set by two corners, popups that keep the player walking and fade, the mid-line text editor, small popups dragged and sized anywhere, OPEN LINK, TAKE ME THERE, and the path keys G, Z and Delete. Each frame is the 480 × 270 GUI-pixel screen (1920 × 1080 at GUI scale 4); the large ones are drawn 1.5 times bigger. Tutorials, places and numbers are samples; the world is a drawn stand-in. Confirm this page (or say what to change) and the build starts at SU-137.

1. The tree builder (AV A)

A tutorial's tree, a point picked

OPEN BUILDER on the TUTORIAL tab opens this. Tabs are the tutorials (★ the first-join one, D720). Nodes are dragged on a canvas you pan and zoom; dragging from one node onto another links them. The drawer shows the picked point: its popup, its place and radius, its links with DRAW PATH or REDRAW, and its buttons. A red dot marks what is missing; PUBLISH asks again while any are left (D734).

A tutorial's own settings, and a start area

TUTORIAL SETTINGS swaps the drawer for the tutorial's settings: its name, how it starts (on first join, by an area, or only with /tutorial), who the first-join one is offered to (new players only at first, D721, D743), its area, and the offer's text. Only one tutorial can be the first-join one; choosing it here takes it from the other.
SET AREA closes the builder and starts area mode (D737), with the same strip and keys as path mode: G sets a corner where you stand (corner 1, then corner 2), Z undoes the last, Delete cancels. The box shows in faint violet to admins in the builder or in area mode; players never see it. With both corners set, SAVE AREA returns to the builder.

2. The popup editor (AW A)

A full-screen popup, with a button's job being picked

EDIT POPUP opens this. The popup's settings across the top; the 12 × 8 grid with the picked box's text editor under it; the preview and the buttons on the right. In the editor, selected letters take bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, the obfuscated effect (▒) and a colour; S, M, L, XL and the alignment apply to the paragraph (D740). Clicking a button opens its label, colour and job; GO TO BRANCH lists this point's branches and is off when it does not fork; OPEN LINK asks for the address.

A small popup that keeps the player walking

SMALL turns the grid to 6 × 4 and the preview into the whole screen, where the popup is dragged anywhere and sized by its corner (at least 80 × 30, snapped to the edges and centre lines, kept as shares of the screen; D727, D741). KEEPS WALKING swaps the buttons for the time on screen, 2 to 60 seconds (D739). A small popup can still freeze the player instead, and then has buttons like a full one.

3. What the player sees (AX A)

The offer, a full popup, a walking popup, a stop on another server

The offer: after the rules popup on first join, or on walking into a start area (D722, D738). LATER asks again at the next login (or the next time into the area); NO THANKS leaves the chat line.
A popup that keeps the player walking: no keys, a bar that empties, then a 1-second fade, and the next point's path shows.
A full popup that freezes the player: the tutorial's name, the branch, the stop on this branch and a thin progress line; END TUTORIAL apart on the left (it reads SURE? for three seconds after the first press), the moving-on keys on the right in the admin's colours.
A next stop on another server (D730): its NEXT reads TAKE ME THERE and moves the player through BSP-Teleport. Without BSP-Teleport the key is off and the line says which server to go to; the tutorial picks up there.

4. Drawing a path (AY A)

DRAW PATH moves the admin to the link's first point (a move through BSP-Teleport when it is on another server). The strip waits for G.
Recording: a point every 3 blocks walked or flown, block-centred, at most 500 (D731). The strip counts points and blocks and how far is left; the keys are under it (D742).
Inside the next point's sphere: the screen's edge glows and the card asks to finish. KEEP WALKING closes it and goes on recording; walking out and back in asks again.

5. The player's golden path (AZ A)

Gold dashes about a block above the walked line, faint through walls, fading with distance (D732); a translucent sign on a thin post at the end names the next stop. Seen only by the player on the tutorial.

What this page fixes for the build

  1. Where things open. /su gets a TUTORIAL tab: the tutorials, each one's start, its counts (started, finished, ended at each point; D735), OPEN BUILDER and PUBLISH. The builder and the popup editor are their own full-screen screens (D719). /su tutorial opens the builder.
  2. Tutorials. Several; one may be the first-join one, the others start by an area or only with /tutorial (D720). The first-join one is offered to new players only until the admin chooses "everyone who has not finished it" (D721, D743).
  3. The offer (D722, D738): YES, LATER, NO THANKS. After the rules popup is answered, or straight after login when there is nothing to agree to; never while the rules hold the player. An area offers once per walk-in; NO THANKS stops that area for that player; a finished tutorial is never offered again.
  4. Points: a title, an item icon, a popup, and a place (server, world, x y z) with a radius of 1 to 8 blocks, 2 by default (D729). Only the player's next point triggers. A point with no place opens its popup as soon as the one before moves on.
  5. Popups: full (12 × 8 grid) or small (6 × 4, dragged and sized anywhere; D724, D727, D741); freezing (with up to four buttons) or walking (no buttons, 2 to 60 s then a 1-second fade; not on a point that forks; D723, D739). Boxes are text (the mid-line editor, D740) or an image (a file from config/bsp_server_utilities/tutorial_images/ or a web address, fitted inside, filled or stretched; D726).
  6. Buttons (D728): a label, a colour, and NEXT, PREVIOUS, END TUTORIAL (SURE? within three seconds), CLOSE (hide and walk on), GO TO BRANCH (one child of a fork) or OPEN LINK (the game's confirm-link screen). A point that forks needs a GO TO BRANCH per child before PUBLISH is quiet. A NEXT to another server or world reads TAKE ME THERE (D730).
  7. Paths (D731, D742): DRAW PATH on a link whose two points share a server and world; the admin is moved to the first; G starts and pauses, Z undoes, Delete cancels; a point every 3 blocks, at most 500; FINISH PATH, KEEP WALKING or UNDO LAST inside the next sphere. Admins see the yellow line and the spheres only in path mode.
  8. The player's path (D732): gold dashes 0.9 blocks above the line, faint through walls, fading past about 50 blocks; the sign on a post at the end. Shader packs get the see-through fallback BSP-Core's X-ray uses.
  9. Progress (D733, D734): kept for the network per player; a logout or a server change resumes at the point reached; /tutorial offers RESUME or START AGAIN, and lists the tutorials a player may start. Edits are a draft until PUBLISH; players mid-way continue at the same point, or end with a chat line if it was deleted.
  10. Records (D735, D736): publishing, deleting a point or a tutorial, drawing a path and setting an area go to the action log as "tutorial"; the setup guide gets a TUTORIAL page after RULES with an off switch, off until a tutorial is first published. Streamer Mode lists the builder and path mode as screens that show positions, hidden by default.
