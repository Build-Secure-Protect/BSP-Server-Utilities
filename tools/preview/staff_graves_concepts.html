Staff panel: mock-up set P, graves

Three looks for a player's graves (D295 to D300): the Graves page under More (a player typed by name, online or not; the same view opens from the Graves key in a player's sheet or card). Each grave says where it is, how old, how much it holds, and whether it is in the world, not placed or put away; RETURN only on graves that can be returned, for Admins and Owners, with a second tap. The items come once InventorySync's SU-IS3 is built. Phone frames; on a computer the same view fills the middle of the dashboard. Made-up names and items.

A. Cards with a list of items

One card per grave, newest first. A card opens to list its items by name with their counts (enchantments and what is inside a backpack under them), then RETURN TO VEXA.

A1, the graves
A2, a grave opened, RETURN tapped once

B. Slots like a chest

Each grave shows its items as slots, like a chest in the game, so a full grave reads at a glance; tap a slot for its name. After RETURN the card says what happened: returned now, or at their next login.

B1, the graves
B2, after RETURN (they are offline)

C. A timeline of deaths

Deaths down a timeline with the time on the left, one line each; tap one and a sheet rises with the items and RETURN, as a player's sheet does.

C1, the timeline
C2, a grave tapped

Choices

  1. P1. The look. A: cards that open to a list of items by name, the clearest about what is in it. B: slots like a chest, a full grave read at a glance. C: a timeline, the easiest to see when someone died again and again.
  2. P2. Before SU-IS3 is built (no items yet). The card says "14 stacks · 23 XP" and "The items show once BSP-InventorySync can list them". Or it says only the counts.
