Bounties on the staff panel

Mock-up set AD, three looks for More › Bounties (D524 to D534). It has OPEN (every running bounty with its target, reward, bonus and window, end, placer, hunters, fee and private notes), HISTORY (the newest 500 closed ones) and + ADD (a bounty paid by the server). Admins, Owners and Moderators with the bounty power add, remove (with or without a refund), extend and blank notes, each with a reason and a SURE? naming what moves. A player's sheet gets a line "Bounties: 1 on them, 2 placed" that opens this page filtered to them. The data is made up.

A. One list with page keys, the bounty opening under its row

Like the Bank & Market page. Tapping a bounty opens its details and keys under it. Chips and a search narrow the list. On a computer the page is one column, 720 px wide.

Choices

  1. AD1 · Which look. Default: A, one list with OPEN, HISTORY and + ADD keys, the bounty opening under its row. Or B: wanted posters, with each bounty on its own screen. Or C: a table with the bounty beside it on a computer, and a bottom sheet on a phone.
  2. AD2 · Narrowing OPEN. Default: chips All, Ending within a day, Staff bounties and With a bonus, plus a search box matching the target, the placer or a hunter. Or: the search box only.
  3. AD3 · The bonus. Default: “+5,000 if killed within 18 h 40 min”, counting down until the window closes, then “bonus window over”. Or: the bonus amount and the time its window ends, as a clock time.
  4. AD4 · History rows. Default: one line each with a coloured ending chip (CLAIMED teal, EXPIRED and CANCELLED grey, REMOVED red, TARGET_STAFF violet, a newer one grey as it is). Tapping a row shows the whole bounty as it was, notes included. Or: every detail shown on every row.
