The AFK tab, three looks

Mock-up set D for AFK detection (D70 to D77): a fourth tab in /su holding the settings for the whole network (D76): whether idle players are marked AFK, after how many minutes (5, D71), whether players AFK too long are kicked (off until turned on, D74) and after how long (30, at the limit whether the server is full or not, D75), and the kick message. Staff and operators are never kicked. Each look also shows who is AFK now. Sample data.

A. Two columns, like STAFF and LOG

The settings on the left (switches, − / + steppers, the kick message), everyone AFK now on the right across the network with how long and where; a line warns who is close to a kick. Same layout as the other admin tabs.

What players see (the same in every look)

A line in their own chat when they go AFK and when they come back (D73), and, if kicking is on, the disconnect screen with the admin's message. /afk marks a player AFK at once (D77). Staff who go AFK show the amber "away" on panels and boards (D72).

Decisions

  1. D1 the tab's look: A two columns, B one form with explanations, or C two cards and a strip.
  2. D2 who is AFK now: listed in the tab, or left out.
  3. D3 the steppers' range: AFK after 1 to 60 minutes and kick after 5 to 240.
  4. D4 the kick message: set by the admin in the tab, up to 160 characters.
