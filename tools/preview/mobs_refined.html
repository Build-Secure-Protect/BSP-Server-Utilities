To confirm

  1. R1 OFF: each number goes down past 1 to OFF; every chunk starts OFF, and a dimension's or single chunk's line is taken away with ×. With everything OFF nothing is refused and the list still counts (default). Or every chunk starts at a number you name now.
  2. R2 which mobs REMOVE EXTRAS takes: the most numerous kind first, then the next, until the chunk is at its limit; tamed and name-tagged mobs never; the key and SURE? say how many of which (default). Or the newest mobs first, whatever their kind.
  3. R3 the fullest list: the 8 fullest chunks on the network with at least 10 mobs, every server sending its own every 5 seconds; a server not heard from for 30 seconds dimmed, dropped after 2 minutes (default). Or 15 rows, scrolling.
