To choose

  1. AB1 the page: look A, two columns like the MOBS page (default); look B, the numbers in one strip over a wide table; look C, an items card and an arrows card.
  2. AB2 the time shown: how long the chunk has been over, with a bar filling towards the clear, "3:24" (default); or how long until it is cleared, "cleared in 1:36".
  3. AB3 OFF: each number goes down past 1 to OFF, so items or arrows alone can be left alone (default); or both are always on.
  4. AB4 the tab key: ENTITIES (default); or MOBS kept as the key's name, with the pages under it.
