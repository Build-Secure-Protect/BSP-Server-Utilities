The LAG tab: three options (set BA)

The LAG tab sits under World in the staff screen's tab list (D746). It has two halves: Loaded, every loaded chunk on this server and the network with its entities and block entities, and Profiler, a timed sample showing which chunks cost the most tick time and the blocks and entities inside them. Each option is drawn twice: the Loaded half, then the Profiler after a 30-second sample (D749). Every frame is a 1920 × 1080 window at GUI scale 4; bigger windows show more rows. Sample data.

Set BA: the LAG tab

A. Two halves as keys across the top, each a full-width table, the picked row's details in a strip below

Loaded: a row of totals, then a table sorted by any column (entities here). SERVER and DIMENSION filter it. TP on each row goes to the chunk (D750); the picked chunk's details sit in the strip under the table.

Profiler: the server, the length (10, 30 or 60 s) and RUN SAMPLE above the ranked chunks, each with ms per tick and a bar of its share of the 50 ms tick. Picking one lists its blocks and entities underneath. "Not in any chunk" is world generation, saving and the network.

B. A list of chunks on the left, the picked chunk opened up on the right

Loaded: the left list is compact (where, entities, block entities). The right side shows the picked chunk in full: why it is loaded and by whom, its entities by type, its busiest block entities, TP, and SAMPLE THIS SERVER.

Profiler: the same two sides, with the list ranked by ms per tick. The right side shows the chunk's blocks and entities by cost, each with TP (standing beside the block, D750). OUTLINE puts a red box on the top 10 for five minutes (D751).

C. A map of chunks around a place, coloured by entities or by tick time, with a side panel

Loaded: a grid of the loaded chunks around a centre (you, or the busiest area), each square coloured by entities, block entities or why it is loaded. Click a square for its details on the right; drag to move; the wheel zooms. A list view is one key away.

Profiler: the same map coloured by ms per tick, so clusters of lag stand out (a farm, a machine room). The side panel ranks the picked chunk's blocks; a strip under the map lists the worst chunks anywhere, to jump to.

The same in every option

  • Other servers' rows come when the tab opens, answered within a few seconds (D748); a server not heard from is greyed. TP to another server goes through BSP-Teleport, greyed without it.
  • A sample on another server runs there and its results come back here. Only one sample runs per server at a time; a second editor sees it running and its results.
  • Editors only (D747). Teleports and samples are in the Action Log (D752).
  • The profiler times block entities and entities by position, and each chunk's own tick (random ticks, spawning). It costs nothing outside a sample.
