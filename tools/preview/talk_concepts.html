Sets AM, AN, AO and AP: private staff messages

Three looks each for the private window in game (set AM, as the player and as staff see it), the popup when staff write (set AN), the MESSAGE STAFF key in the inventory (set AO) and the MESSAGES section and list on the staff panel (set AP). Already decided and drawn in all of them: one conversation per player shared by all staff, each staff line naming its writer (D631); END CONVERSATION for any staff member, with SURE? within 3 seconds (D632, D638); the popup a small centred card with OPEN and LATER plus a clickable chat line (D635); the key right of SUPPORT BSP, flashing red while the newest line is from staff (D634); 256 characters a line (D642). Pick one look per set; you may combine. Sample data: every name and line is made up.

Opened by OPEN on the popup, the MESSAGE STAFF key, the chat line or /su talk (D637). The player's window on the left of each pair; on the right the same window as staff see it after /su talk Wren_88 (D638): the player's name, where they are, and END CONVERSATION. Drawn at 1.5 times its size in game, over a 480 × 270 GUI window. The box takes up to 256 characters; Enter sends.

Set AN: the popup when staff write

A small centred card that takes the mouse like the rules popup (D635), with the chat line under it; it waits while the player is in another screen or was hurt in the last 10 seconds. At the next login after a message sent while they were away, the same card comes 5 seconds after the network join with its words changed (D636). LATER closes it; the key keeps flashing. Drawn at 1.5 times.

Set AP: the staff panel

The MESSAGES section in a player's card on a computer (the Players tab, D640) and in their sheet on a phone; the same section on an offline player's page (More › Find a player). Under each, the list of open conversations under More › Player messages, with a red count on More for replies nobody has answered (D639). END CONVERSATION asks SURE? within 3 seconds. Staff lines carry the writer's name; the newest is at the foot; 90 days are kept (D643).
