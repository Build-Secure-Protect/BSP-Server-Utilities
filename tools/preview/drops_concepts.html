The DROPS tab, three looks

Mock-up set F for despawn times (D101 to D110): a sixth tab in /su with the times for the network (items 5 minutes, death drops 15, XP orbs as items, counted while the server runs whether or not anyone is near), the exceptions list (an item with its own time or never, added through an item search), how many were removed in the last hour, and CLEAR ITEMS for this server, every server or one picked (also /su clearitems here | all | <server>). The title is drawn a little smaller to make room for six tabs. Sample data.

The clear, in chat (every look)

With the default warning (F3): the players on the servers being cleared see a red line, then the gold one; the admin gets the count from each server.

Items on the ground will be cleared in 30 seconds.Items on the ground were cleared (312).[to the admin] Cleared: BSP-Spawn 312 items · BSP-Wild 98 · BSP-Nether 0

Decisions

  1. F1 the tab's look: A two columns, B one form, or C three cards.
  2. F2 what the clear removes: dropped items and XP orbs, or items only.
  3. F3 a warning: 30 seconds' notice in chat on the servers being cleared, or cleared at once.
  4. F4 unloaded chunks: items in chunks not loaded at the time go when their chunk next loads, or only loaded ones are cleared.
  5. F5 who may clear: operators and BSP-Core admins, or staff too.
