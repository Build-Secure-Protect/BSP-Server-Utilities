Staff panel: mock-up set W, network bans

Three looks for banning a player and the bans list (D255, D350 to D359): 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, custom or permanent; a reason the player sees (up to 160 characters) and an optional note only staff see; BAN asks twice (D357); the More › Bans page with ACTIVE and PAST, UNBAN with SURE? and EDIT. Admins and Owners ban; only the Owner bans staff. Opened from BAN in a player's sheet or card, or + BAN on the Bans page. Made-up names.

A. A form, then type their name

One sheet: length chips, the reason, the staff note, earlier bans in gold. BAN turns into SURE?; the second check asks for the player's name typed out before the red BAN key wakes up. The list is cards with the time left, UNBAN and EDIT.

A1, the form, BAN tapped once (SURE?)
A2, the second check: type the name
A3, More › Bans

B. Steps, a preview, hold to ban

Three short steps (how long, why, check). The check page shows the screen the player will get when they try to join. BAN asks SURE?, then the key must be held down for two seconds. The list is compact rows with a bar for the time left.

B1, step 2: the reason
B2, step 3: the preview, held halfway
B3, More › Bans

C. One page, a summary to confirm

The player's screen previewed live at the top while you fill the form under it. The first BAN (outlined) brings up a red summary (who, how long, when it ends, the reason) with YES, BAN VEXA: the second check is a different key in a different place, so a double tap cannot do both. The list is grouped by when bans end.

C1, the form with the live preview
C2, the summary: the second check
C3, More › Bans, grouped

Choices

  1. W1. The look. A: one form sheet and cards. B: three steps with a preview of the player's screen, and compact rows. C: one page with the live preview, and a list grouped by when bans end.
  2. W2. The two checks (any look can take any of these). First SURE?, then typing the player's name (A). First SURE?, then holding the key for two seconds (B). The outlined BAN, then a red summary with a different YES key (C).
  3. W3. The preview of the player's screen. Shown on the form, as in B and C. Or not shown (the text is fixed: D354).
