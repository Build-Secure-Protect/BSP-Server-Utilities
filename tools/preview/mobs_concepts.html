Set AA: mob limits

Three looks for the MOBS tab (D478). The rules drawn are the ones you chose: mobs only, anything alive but players (D471); every limit is a number of mobs in one chunk, with the chunk's own number winning over its dimension's, and the dimension's over the blanket one (D472); new mobs refused at the limit from every source, mobs walking in still counting (D473, D474); the fullest chunks on every server with REMOVE EXTRAS on this server only, SURE? first, pets and name-tagged mobs never removed (D475, D479); blanket and dimension limits for the whole network, a single chunk naming its server (D476); an exempt list (D477). Pick a look; you may combine. Sample data: a blanket limit of 40, 25 in Compact Machines rooms, your server names; nothing here is live.

Look A: two columns, limits on the left, the fullest chunks on the right

Like the AFK tab: everything you set on the left (the blanket number, each dimension, single chunks, the exempt mobs), and on the right the fullest chunks on every server, worst first. Pick a row to see it below the list; REMOVE EXTRAS shows only for a chunk on this server. LIMIT on a row adds that chunk to the single chunks with its own number; HERE adds the chunk you stand in.

What players and the log see (every look)

A player whose breeding, spawn egg or summon was refused gets one line on the action bar (D474). Natural spawns and spawners are refused silently. Removing extras goes to the Action Log, kind "Mobs", staff only.

To choose

  1. AA1 the tab: look A, two columns with the limits beside the fullest chunks (default); look B, three pages inside the tab (Limits, Fullest, Never counted); look C, one list of rules, most specific first, over a strip of bars.
  2. AA2 adding a single chunk: HERE for the chunk you stand in, and LIMIT on any row of the fullest chunks, which adds that chunk on its own server with its current limit to change (default); or HERE only.
  3. AA3 where a chunk is named: in the room dimension by the room's owner ("room · Steve_42"), elsewhere by dimension and chunk coordinates, with block coordinates on hover (default); or always chunk and block coordinates.
  4. AA4 adding a dimension: ADD lists every dimension any server of the network has, by its name (default); or a box to type its id.
