The TOTEMS tab on the staff panel

Mock-up set AJ, three looks for the new TOTEMS tab between Map and Staff (D613 to D622), for every staff rank. Each look holds the same things: the board (points, totems held and best tier, points gained in the window), "stealing now" (live), every steal with where, when, from whom, how long it took against BSP-Core's time and the time since that player's last steal, and a player's own view with their points over time. Flags: FAST (finished more than 2 s sooner than BSP-Core allows) and BURST (3 completed steals within 10 minutes). Windows: 24 h, 7 days (the default), 30 days, 90 days.

A. Watch list first: the flagged players at the top, then the board, then the steals

One column that reads top to bottom. "Stealing now" stays at the top while anything runs. Next come the players with flags in the window, each with their counts. The board follows, with the points gained in the window, and the steals as a list under it. Tapping a player opens their view under the row: their points line, then their steals. On a computer the column is 760 px wide.

C. A timeline: everyone's points over the window, steals as dots on it

The page opens on one chart with the leading players' points over the window. Each steal is a dot on the thief's line: red when it was fast, gold in a burst, hollow when it failed. Under the chart, one feed in time order holds the steals and the other changes of points. The board is a strip of chips above the chart, the leader first. Tapping a line or a name narrows the feed to that player.

Choices

  1. AJ1 · Which look. Default: A, one column with stealing now, the flagged players, the board with gains, then the steals, and a player opening under their row with their points line. Or B: three panes on a computer (board, every steal, the picked player), and three keys on a phone. Or C: a chart of the top players' points with every steal as a dot, and one feed of steals and points changes under it.
  2. AJ2 · How a steal's speed reads. Default: the time it took beside BSP-Core's time ("0:19 of 0:40"), red when FAST, and under it "1 min 41 s after their last". Or: a percentage of BSP-Core's time ("48 %"), with the gap in its own column.
  3. AJ3 · Where. Default: "Frostwarf · Nether · 118 64 -874", with MAP › opening the Map tab on that server and world at that block (D621). Or: the raw world id ("minecraft:the_nether") and the coordinates with x, y and z labels.
  4. AJ4 · Failed steals. Default: shown in the list dimmed, with a FAILED tag and BSP-Core's reason in words, and counted beside each player (D615). Or: hidden until a Failed chip is picked.
  5. AJ5 · The FAST warning. Default: one gold line in a player's view that a FAST steal may be an operator pressing finish on BSP-Core's admin screen, which is not in the Action Log, until BSP-Core can tell them apart (SU-BC4). Or: no warning line.
  6. AJ6 · Steals that could not be verified (a row without a good signature). Default: a gold line at the top of the steals, such as "2 steals could not be verified and are not shown", only when there are any. Or: nothing shown.
