To confirm

  1. R1 · The posters' order. Default: newest first, as BSP-Core gives them. Or: ending soonest first.
  2. R2 · The reason on a bounty's screen. Default: one “Why” box above the keys; every key stays dimmed until a reason is typed. +1 HOUR, +1 DAY, +7 DAYS and BLANK NOTES act on one tap; DAYS… asks for a number and a SURE?. REMOVE keys always need a SURE? (D530, D532). Or: every key, the quick ones too, needs a SURE?.
  3. R3 · After a change. Default: after a remove the page goes back to the board, with the answer at the top. After an extend or a blank it stays on the bounty, read again, with the answer under the keys. Or: it always stays on the bounty's screen, showing "This bounty has ended" after a remove.
  4. R4 · A coin bounty's poster. Default: the count with the tier's coloured dot and name (“3 ◆ diamond”). Or: also its worth in pieces, from the coin values.
