Rules popup: three looks, and the RULES tab: three looks

Set A of the mock-ups for BSP-Server Utilities. The popup shows once per player for the network (D7), after their inventory has arrived; the player is held until they tick every rule and press AGREE (D8, D9); Escape does nothing and LEAVE disconnects (D10). A rule is a bold title with optional longer text (D11). Drawn at one and a half times the in-game size over a darkened world; the rules are sample text, the real ones are yours.

A2. The RULES tab of the admin panel (violet)

Look A: list and editor

/su opens the panel on RULES. The list on the left has a POPUP TEXT row for the heading and intro, then the rules, then + ADD RULE. The right side edits the picked row: title, text (wraps as it will in the popup), UP, DOWN, DELETE (SURE? within three seconds). The footer says which version is live and how many players agreed to it; PREVIEW opens the popup as a player sees it (closing it changes nothing); ASK EVERYONE AGAIN raises the version after SURE?. Edits save at once and reach every server.

Decisions for set A (defaults first)

  1. A1. The popup's look. Default: look A, one scrolling checklist with the text under each title. Or look B, the contents list with a reading pane. Or look C, one rule at a time with NEXT.
  2. A2. AGREE before every box is ticked. Default: AGREE is shown dim with "Tick every rule to agree" beside it, and clicking it does nothing. Or AGREE is hidden until the last box is ticked.
  3. A3. LEAVE. Default: a red outline key at the bottom left, far from AGREE, that disconnects at once with your message. Or LEAVE asks SURE? first.
  4. A4. The RULES tab's look. Default: look A, a list beside an editor, with PREVIEW. Or look B, rule cards edited in place. Or look C, the form beside a live preview.
  5. A5. ASK EVERYONE AGAIN. Default: a red key in the RULES tab footer that needs SURE? within three seconds, then raises the version so every player sees the popup at their next join. Or a command only (/su rules askagain).
  6. A6. Limits. Default: up to 20 rules, a title up to 48 characters, a rule's text up to 400 characters, the heading up to 32 and the intro up to 160; the popup scrolls when the rules do not fit. Or no limits beyond what the screen can show.
  7. A7. What the footer counts. Default: the live version and how many players have agreed to it, across the network. Or the version only.
