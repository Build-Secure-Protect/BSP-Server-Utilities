Staff panel: mock-up set T, notes and tags

Three looks for a player's notes and tags in their sheet (D266, D267, D279): notes seen by all staff with author and time, edited or deleted only by their author or an admin; tags from a list admins keep with colours, beside the name everywhere in the panel, and any staff member may type a new one on the spot. Each look also draws the admins' tag list (under More). Below, three looks for the notes count in game when staff hover a player. Made-up names.

A. A section in the sheet

Tags sit under the name at the top of the sheet with a dashed + TAG; notes are a section of cards further down (where D279 placed them), each with its author, rank and time, a ⋯ for its author or an admin, and a box to add one at the foot. + TAG opens a picker: tick tags from the list, or type a new one. Under More, admins see the tags as a list with a colour, how many players carry each, and the new tags typed by staff waiting for a colour.

B. A Notes page in the sheet

The sheet's own switch (All, Items, Notes, Log) gets the notes as a page: a timeline of notes and tag changes, newest first, so "who tagged her and why" reads in one place. Writing a note fills the sheet, with the tags to add underneath. Admins' tag list as coloured cards.

B1, the Notes page: notes and tag changes
B2, writing a note, adding a tag with it
B3, More › Tags as cards

C. The newest note pinned on top

The newest note sits in a gold box at the very top of the sheet, above the stats, so nobody misses it; tap it for every note. Tags under the name carry a small ✕ to take them off. Admins' tag list as a table with a colour picker under the opened tag.

Choices

  1. T1. The look in the sheet. A: tags under the name and a notes section of cards further down, a picker for tags, the admins' list as rows. B: a Notes page in the sheet's switch, a timeline of notes and tag changes, the admins' list as coloured cards. C: the newest note pinned in a gold box at the top of the sheet, tags with a ✕, the admins' list as a table.
  2. T2. The hover in game. H1: "2 notes", then who wrote the newest and how long ago. H2: the same plus the newest note's words, cut to three lines. H3: a small gold ✎ with the count on every row that has notes, and the player's tags in colour in the hover.
  3. T3. A tag typed on the spot. It joins the list for everyone at once, grey, until an admin gives it a colour (or removes it). Or it stays on that one player only, until an admin adds it to the list.
  4. T4. A note's length. Up to 1000 characters of plain text. Or up to 300.
  5. T5. Editing a note. An "edited 21:40" mark under it, and the earlier words kept in the panel's own log. Or the note is simply replaced, with no mark.
  6. T6. Taking a tag off a player. Any staff member. Or only whoever put it on, and admins.
  7. T7. Where notes and tag changes are recorded. In the panel's own log only (they are about players, not done to them). Or in the game's Action Log as well, with "web" as every panel action.
