The CLIENTS tab, three looks

Mock-up set G for the client check (D120 to D134): a seventh tab in /su. It lists every online player on the network with what their client reported at login (mods, resource packs, shader, the stone test, brightness), their flags, and KICK with a typed reason. It also holds the rules: the block list, the allowed-anyway list, exempt players, the refusal message, the trusted setup (TRUST MY SETUP, EXPORT) and the switch between Network rules and This server's own rules. The seven tab keys are drawn narrower to fit. Sample data.

A. Two pages: PLAYERS and RULES

A switch under the title. PLAYERS: the network's players on the left (the flag count in gold, refusals today below), the picked player on the right with MODS / PACKS, BLOCK beside each line, and KICK at the foot. RULES: the page drawn under "The rules page".

Decisions

  1. G1 the tab's look: A two pages (PLAYERS and RULES), B one page in three columns, or C a rules strip over player rows.
  2. G2 a player kicked by staff: sees "Removed by staff" above the typed reason (as drawn); or sees only the typed reason; or sees the reason with the admin's name under it.
  3. G3 the order of a player's mods: the unusual ones first (not trusted, file differs) in gold, then the rest A to Z; or A to Z only, with the unusual ones in gold where they fall.
  4. G4 refusals in the tab: today's refusals across the network listed under the players (as drawn), also in the Action Log; or in the Action Log only.
