Staff panel: the SHOPS tab: concepts

Four sets, three looks each, for the owner's brief of 2026-11-26, drawn on decisions D788 to D812. BG is the grid of shops. BH is a shop's page with its sections. BI is the editor: the item browser, dragging items onto the page and the card drawer. BJ is making a new shop, copying one, its settings, opening hours and turning it on and off. Pick one look per set, or mix them. Item pictures are drawn stand-ins for the panel's item export; names, prices and places are made up.

BG Shop grid · BH A shop's page · BI The editor · BJ Creator and settings

BG. The shop grid

SHOPS sits in the menu after Worlds. Every look has the search, the filter chips (All, On, Off, Network, Rotating, Offer), + NEW SHOP for Admins and Owners, and on each shop: its name, kind, cards, the ON/OFF switch, the Shopping Network mark, open now or when it opens, and one chip per server with stalls (hovering shows them by kind).

Four tiles across, each with its first four cards drawn small; the OFF shops striped with "OFF: closed for changes"; hovering BSP-Spawn on the General Store splits its six venues by kind

Three tiles across, each topped by a strip in the shop's in-game style (Cash Machine, Passbook, Market Board); kind and the switch on the strip, servers along the foot

BG C. Small tiles in two groups

Five narrow tiles across, ON shops first, then OFF; the opening hours as a 24-hour strip on each tile; a server with no stalls left out

BH. A shop's page and its sections

Opened by clicking a tile. The cards are drawn as players see them: the shop's columns, rows and pages. A section card opens its section in place; hidden cards are dimmed, locked cards carry a padlock with what they need. The stalls, sales (last 7 days by default) and EXPORT live on the same page. EDIT opens the editor (BI); SETTINGS opens BJ.

The General Store: seven cards a row as the shop is set, three section cards first (Food, Tools, Redstone); down the right the settings in brief, the stalls by server, and the sales

The same shop with Tools open: the whole tree of sections on the left with their card counts, the breadcrumb on top, tabs below for Cards, Stalls and Sales

The Challenge (a rotating shop): every card of its pool with its weight, the four on show now lit in teal, the next change and the last three draws

BI. The editor

EDIT on a shop's page (Admins and Owners). Each change is sent as it is made and lands on the game servers within a moment; if the shop changed in game since, the change is refused and the page reloads. Items come from the panel's item pictures, searched by name or id with a chip per creative tab. Drag onto a slot to add, drag a card to move it, drag onto the bin to remove it. Each look shows a different drawer tab, so all three are seen.

BJ. The creator and settings

+ NEW SHOP and COPY ask a name and an id (filled from the name) and make the shop OFF, so it can be filled before players see it. SETTINGS holds every setting the game has. Turning a shop OFF asks once; while it is OFF a grey bar says so on every view of it. DELETE asks SURE? twice.

What every look shares

  1. Where shops come from. One live game server answers for every shop, since BSP-Core shares them over the network; each server answers for its own stalls, posts and displays. Nothing reads BSP-Core's tables directly.
  2. Who may change things. Every rank sees everything; Admins and Owners create, edit, copy, turn on and off and delete. Moderators see the editor's keys marked "Admin" and greyed.
  3. ON and OFF. An OFF shop is left off the Shopping Network and opens for no player anywhere (stalls show closed, "closed for changes"); its sections go off with it. Staff still edit it here and test it in game. New shops and copies start OFF.
  4. Safety. Each change carries the shop's edit stamp, so an edit made in game meanwhile is never overwritten. Moving or removing cards restarts their counts, as in game, and the page says so. Every change goes in the panel's log and the game's Action Log.
  5. Waiting on BSP-Core. The tab reads and changes shops through a shop api BSP-Core has yet to add (requests SU-BC5 to SU-BC10); until then the panel is built against made-up shops.
