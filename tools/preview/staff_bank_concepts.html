A player's Bank & Market on the staff panel

Mock-up set AC, three looks for the page a “Bank & Market” key opens on a player's sheet (D505). It holds their pieces, the coins kept in their bank, their bank log (D506) and their open lots and requests. Admins, Owners and a Moderator with the BANK power refund rows and cancel lots and requests (D503, D504), each with a reason (D510) and a SURE? that names what goes out (D509). The data is made up. Amounts are signed from the player's side, as BSP-Core gives them: money in is teal, money out is coral.

A. One sheet with three page keys, the refund opening under its row

The money at the top, then LOG, LOTS and REQUESTS as page keys with their counts. The log is filtered by chips, 50 rows at a time with MORE at the end. Tapping a row opens the refund form under that row, so the row stays in view while the amounts are typed. Lots and requests have CANCEL with the same reason and SURE?. The same page on a computer is one column, 720 px wide.

Choices

  1. AC1 · Which look. Default: A, one sheet with LOG, LOTS and REQUESTS keys and the refund opening under its row. Or B: a money summary with the last 7 days, the log grouped by day, and each refund on a screen of its own. Or C: a three-column ledger on a computer with the refund beside the table, and a bottom sheet on a phone.
  2. AC2 · How the amounts read. Default: signed from the player's side as BSP-Core gives them, teal +320 in and coral −320 out, with coins as a coloured dot per tier after the number. Or: words, such as “paid out 320” and “got 4,560”, with no signs.
  3. AC3 · A row refunded in part. Default: a thin violet bar under the row showing how much of it is refunded, and its REFUND rows saying “of row 1200”. Or: only the line “100 pieces, 1 coin refunded” under the row, with no bar.
  4. AC4 · Rows that can never be refunded (DEPOSIT, WITHDRAW: the player's own coins). Default: shown as other rows, with “their own coins · not refunded” and no refund form when tapped. Or: left out of the log on the panel altogether.
  5. AC5 · How a staff member who may only look sees it. Default: the same page with no refund form and no CANCEL keys, and one line at the top saying “Refunds and cancels are for Admins, Owners and Moderators given the bank power.” Or: the keys shown dimmed with an Admin badge, as the Broadcast entry in More is.
