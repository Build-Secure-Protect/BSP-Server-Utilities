The points to confirm

  1. R1 the list.
    • The OFFLINE group sits under every online group, in both the Teams and Servers views, with the newest seen first.
    • Each row has a faded head, the name in light grey (#aab0bc, easy to read on the dark panel), tags, then "server · world" (the Overworld left out, as for online players), and on the right how long ago.
    • "How long ago" reads "12 min", "3 h", "yest.", "4 d" up to 13 days, then the date ("18 Oct"), with the year once it is not this year. A row with no record shows "—" and "not recorded", after everyone with a record, by name.
    • On a computer there is an "Offline" tile with the count, and the count line reads "12 online · 3 teams · 1,284 offline". At the foot: "SHOW 50 MORE · 5 OF 1,284".
    • The group folds like a team group, and folded it stays folded in this browser.
  2. R2 the search.
    • Typing filters the online groups as now, and the OFFLINE group then lists every offline player whose name holds those letters, up to 50, read from the panel's database a quarter of a second after the last key.
    • Its header reads "3 match “ju”", or "Nobody offline matches “ju”".
  3. R3 the phone. The OFFLINE group comes at the foot with 20 rows and SHOW MORE, and the server chips end with a grey "Offline 1,284" chip. A tap opens the card as the bottom sheet.
  4. R4 the card.
    • Head and name are greyed, with an OFFLINE chip and "last seen 12 min ago (21:04)"; older than yesterday it reads "last seen 18 Nov 22:10".
    • In the box: "○ Logged off on BSP-Main · nether", then "120, 64, -30 · 21:04" and MAP ›, which opens the Map tab exactly as R7 does.
    • Keys: Graves, Bank, Message and Ban. Then MESSAGES, the market and bounties lines, INVENTORY "saved 12 min ago · BSP-Main" (changes reach them at their next login) and NOTES.
    • The states drawn above:
      • A crash: "Last seen on …", with a gold "the server did not record a logout" line.
      • No record: "not recorded yet. It is kept from their next logout."
      • A failed signed copy: "Last place not known".
      • A world whose map is not shown: MAP › greyed, with a gold line saying so.
      • Logging on while open: it becomes their live card.
  5. R5 Find a player. An offline result opens the same card on the Players tab, and the old offline page goes.
  6. R6 the map's switch.
    • "Show offline players" sits under the window's search, off the first time and then remembered in this browser.
    • When on, a grey OFFLINE heading with the count comes after the servers. Rows have a faded head, a light-grey name, how long ago in short form ("12m", "3h", "1d", "4d", "2w", "3mo", or "—") and the world tag. Newest first, 50, then MORE.
    • The window's search filters the offline rows by any part of the name, as R2 does. The window's count stays the online count.
  7. R7 choosing an offline player on the map.
    • Anyone being followed stops being followed. The map switches to their server and world and centres on the block.
    • A pin goes there: their head in grey with a dashed ring, labelled "Name · logged off". It stays until another player is chosen or the card is closed.
    • The corner card (a small sheet on a phone) shows OFFLINE, "logged off 12 min ago (21:04)", "server · world · x, y, z", the HOTBAR from the newest save, then ITEMS (the whole inventory), NOTES, and OPEN CARD (the R4 card on the Players tab).
    • A world whose map is not shown: the card says so and the map stays where it was. No record: the card with "not recorded", and no pin.
  8. R8 keeping up to date.
    • The offline list is read when the tab opens, a few seconds after anyone leaves the network (their logout is written first), and every 60 seconds while the Players tab, or the map with the switch on, is shown.
    • Someone who logs on moves to their online group at once, from the live feed.
    • Every rank (Moderator, Admin, Owner) sees all of it.
  9. R9 the words. "Offline", "last seen 12 min ago (21:04)", "Logged off on …", "Last seen on …", "The server did not record a logout: this is its last 5-minute record.", "Where they logged off is not recorded yet.
