The setup guide, three looks

Mock-up set H for the setup guide (D143 to D154): the popup an admin sees a few seconds after joining (after the rules popup and the inventory restore) while anything is not set up and they have not ignored it, and the guide it opens. Each page of the guide is that feature's existing /su tab, shown here with the RULES tab; the footer is BACK, TURN OFF FEATURE, SETUP LATER and NEXT. Pages, in order: Welcome, Staff groups, Rules, Action log, AFK, Chunk unloading, Despawn times, Client check, Display panels, Finish. Sample data.

Decisions

  1. H1 the popup's look: P-A a card in the middle that blocks the game, P-B a card in the corner that does not, or P-C the checklist.
  2. H2 the guide's look: G-A step dots in the title row, G-B a list of pages on the left, or G-C the normal panel with a guide strip.
  3. H3 BACK: every page but Welcome has BACK (as drawn), or the guide only goes forward.
  4. H4 closing the guide part way (Escape): the pages not reached count as later and the admin is reminded at their next join, or nothing is recorded and the guide starts again from Welcome.
