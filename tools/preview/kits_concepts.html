Staff panel: the KITS tab (mock-up set BM)

One mock-up, modelled on the SHOPS tab as built: tiles in a grid, a kit's page, an editor with the item browser on the left and a drawer on the right, a NEW KIT window, two confirms for anything that cannot be undone. K1 to K7 below; the list at the foot says what each does. Item pictures are drawn stand-ins; kit names, ranks, players and counts are made up.

Works on BSP-Core 1.27.0 (kit api part 1: details, detail, choices, KitEvent.Changed): reading every kit whole, the pickers, refreshing on a change. Waits on BSP-Core (kit api parts 2 and 3; requests SU-BC11 to SU-BC13): every change, copy, delete, give, a player's records. Drawn here so the whole tab can be confirmed once.

What each screen does

  1. K1. The grid. One tile per kit, in the order the kit browser shows them to players (the order they were made): the icon, name and id, the ON/OFF switch (Admins and Owners), the first eight items with their counts and "+4" for the rest, the price (Free or pieces), when it can be taken again (Any time, Once, Every 7 d), hidden from the browser, handed out at first join, the ranks, servers and conditions it needs, how many times it has been taken and by whom last. OFF kits striped. A red line when the answering server cannot give some of its items (a mod not loaded there). Chips filter All, On, Off, In the browser, Hidden, First join, Free; search finds kits by name, id or an item in them. A click opens the kit (K2).
  2. K2. A kit's page. CONTENTS as a 9-wide grid of its stacks (up to the network's limit, 36 here), hovering a stack shows its name, count and data; the card as players see it in the kit browser; RULES (shown or hidden, price, timing, ranks, servers, needs, first join) and RECORD (taken, last taker, made, last edited, and the server that answered). Keys: the switch, COPY, GIVE TO…, DELETE, EDIT; all five wait on BSP-Core, so on 1.27.0 they are greyed with "Needs BSP-Core's kit changes".
  3. K3. The editor, KIT. EDIT turns the page into the editor (Admins and Owners), as the SHOPS card editor: the item browser on the left (the same one, search by English name or id, a chip per creative tab, items with data keep it), the contents in the middle, the drawer on the right. Dropping an item between stacks puts it there and at the end adds it (a kit is a list of stacks, as in game); the wheel or the count box sets a stack's count; dragging a stack moves it. The KIT tab: name (up to 24), description (up to 80, as the browser shows it), icon (an item from the browser, or the first item), shown in the browser, price in pieces (empty for free), timing (Any time, Once, Every N minutes, hours or days). Each change goes to the game as it is made and "Saved" shows; if the kit changed in game since, it is refused and the page reads again with a line saying so.
  4. K4. WHO AND WHERE. Ranks from BSP-Core's pickers: RankUp's ladder as steps ("Miner and up": a click on a step) or, without RankUp, LuckPerms group chips; servers it can be taken on (Everywhere, or one chip per server the network has seen); the conditions BSP-Core offers (today one: owns no Shatter Totem anywhere on the network); FIRST JOIN (Never, Once on the network, On each chosen server, with their chips). Dragging a stack onto the bin asks SURE? REMOVE for three seconds; the stacks after it move up.
  5. K5. New kit and copy. + NEW KIT asks a name and an id (filled from the name: 2 to 24 lower-case letters, digits and _, checked as typed); the kit is made OFF and empty and opens in the editor. COPY asks the same; settings and items are copied, made OFF, with no counts, last taker or players' records.
  6. K6. Give and delete. GIVE TO… picks any player the panel knows, online or not; it lands at once when they are online with room, otherwise it waits for them (a line in chat and a WAITING card in their browser), recorded as an admin give with your name. DELETE asks SURE? for three seconds and says first how many players are still owed it; their records go with it, as in game.
  7. K7. A phone. KITS is in More, the grid one column, a kit's page with its contents and rules; changing a kit on a computer only, as the SHOPS card editor (D814).
  8. Every view. Moderators see everything; the switch, EDIT, + NEW KIT, COPY, GIVE TO… and DELETE are greyed with "Admin". Pages refresh within a few seconds of a change in game (BSP-Core's change event). Every change is in the panel's log and the game's Action Log. Left in game: TEST ON ME, FROM HAND and FROM INVENTORY (they need you in the game).
