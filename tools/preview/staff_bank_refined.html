To confirm

  1. R1 · Where the key sits. Default: a Bank & Market key on the player's sheet beside Graves, on the Players tab and on Find a player, opening LOG. The “On the market” line keeps opening the Market page. Or: the “On the market” line opens this page on LOTS instead, and the Market page is reached only from More.
  2. R2 · Fresh numbers. Default: the page reads the player when it opens, on REFRESH, and again after every refund or cancel, and shows how long ago it read (“read 40 s ago”); nothing re-reads by itself while a form is open. Or: it also re-reads every 30 seconds while open, closing any form that is open.
  3. R3 · How the form starts. Default: FULL REFUND is picked when a row opens, filling in everything left; AN AMOUNT lets each number be typed, never past what is left. For a payment, “Also take it back from <other>” starts on (D508), for PAID and RECEIVED rows alike. Or: AN AMOUNT is picked with the boxes empty, so every refund is typed.
  4. R4 · The filter chips. Default: Payments (PAID, RECEIVED), Market (MARKET, TAX), Shops (BOUGHT, SOLD, RENT, FEE, LOTTERY), Coins (DEPOSIT, CONVERT, WITHDRAW), Staff & refunds (GRANT, ADMIN, REFUND); a type BSP-Core adds later shows under All only. Or: LOTTERY gets a chip of its own.
