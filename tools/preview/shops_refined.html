What each screen does

  1. R1. The grid. One tile per top-level shop, A to Z: name and id, the ON/OFF switch (Admins and Owners), kind, cards and sections, the Shopping Network mark, its first four cards, open now or when it opens (OFF tiles striped, "OFF: closed for changes"), what it needs, and one chip per server with stalls; hovering a chip splits them into Market Stalls, Vendor Posts and Stall Displays. Search finds shops and sections by name or id. A click opens the shop (R2).
  2. R2. The shop's page. The sections as a tree on the left with their card counts; the breadcrumb on top; three tabs: Cards, Stalls, Sales. Cards are drawn as players see them, by the shop's own columns, rows and pages; section cards (blue) open the section in place; hidden cards dimmed with HIDDEN (SHOW HIDDEN turns them off); locked cards with a padlock and what they need. Hovering a card shows its full settings. Keys: the switch, SETTINGS, COPY, EXPORT, EDIT.
  3. R3. Stalls. Every Market Stall, Vendor Post and Stall Display linked to this shop or its sections, by server: kind, world, x y z, the section it serves, its own opening hours or "the shop's", read-only (D806). A server that is off shows its last answer greyed. Stalls not yet seen since BSP-Core started keeping the list show up the first time their chunk loads, which the tab says.
  4. R4. Sales. From BSP-Core's shop report (SU-BC10), 1, 7 or 30 days: pieces in and out and the net, coins in and out, then every card that traded, sorted by pieces, with items bought and sold; for a section, only its cards.
  5. R5. Rotating shops. The same page; a strip above the cards gives the draw: how many on show, when it changes, no repeats and how many are left in the cycle, whether it tells every player, and the last three draws. The pool's cards show their weight, the cards on show now lit.
  6. R6. The editor. EDIT turns the page into the editor (Admins and Owners): the item browser on the left (search by English name or id, a chip per creative tab, items with data keep it; without a picture export, a box for an item id), the page in the middle, the drawer on the right when a card is chosen. Dropping an item between cards puts it there; dragging a card moves it; each change goes to the game as it is made, and "Saved" shows on the drawer. If the shop changed in game since, the change is refused and the page reloads with a line saying so. The CARD tab: lot size (up to a stack), buy and sell (empty for off), paid in (Pieces, Copper, Gold, Diamond, Netherite, Illyrium), the price as players see it, instant, hidden, selling takes the exact item or any data, weight on a rotating shop, the three-line note.
  7. R7. LIMITS. Stock (buying) and sell room (selling), each for everyone and per player, in lots (empty for no limit), with their refill rules as chips (Never, After…, Every day at…, Every weekday at…, Each rotation on a rotating shop, Same as everyone for the per-player ones) and the time zone named; what is left now; REFILL NOW. A change to a limit, a rule, the lot size or the match restarts the card's counts, as in game, and the drawer says so first.
  8. R8. NEEDS and removing. Permission node, hours played, show locked or hide, the reason, and how players will see it. Dragging a card onto the bin under the page asks SURE? REMOVE for three seconds; the cards after it move up and their counts restart.
  9. R9. A new section. + SECTION asks a name, the picture (from the browser) and an id (filled from the shop's id and the name); the section card lands at the end of the page, or where it is dropped. A section has its own settings (R11) like any shop.
  10. R10. A new shop. Name, id (filled from the name, letters, digits and _, up to 32, checked as typed) and kind; made OFF and opened in its settings (R11).
  11. R11. Settings. One page in sections, a jump list on the left: General (name, id read-only, inside, Shopping Network, buy only), Look (style, cards a row, rows, a small picture of each style), Opening hours (Always, Real clock with the time zone, Minecraft day; a 24-hour strip), Rotating (only for a rotating shop: on show, every N hours or a fixed time, no repeats, tell every player), Special offer (only for an offer: percent, ends at, never ends), Needs (as R8, for the whole shop and its sections), Delete. Each field saves as it is changed, as the editor does.
  12. R12. Copy and off. COPY: a new name and id, the sections it copies with their new ids; made OFF, counts and sales start fresh, stalls stay with the first shop; opens in the editor. Turning a shop off asks once, naming what goes off with it; while off, a grey bar on every view of it with TURN ON.
  13. Every view. Moderators see everything; the switch, EDIT, + NEW SHOP, COPY and DELETE are greyed with "Admin". Pages refresh within a few seconds of a change in game. Every change is in the panel's log and the game's Action Log. On a phone SHOPS is in More, the grid one column, a shop's page with the tree as a dropdown, and editing offered on a computer only (D814, proposed).
