The details to confirm

  1. R1 · The tab. Default: TOTEMS sits between Map and Staff for every rank. On a phone the bottom bar holds six tabs. The window key (24 H, 7 DAYS, 30 DAYS, 90 DAYS; 7 days at first) is remembered on this device. Sections in order: stealing now (only while something runs), flagged, board, steals.
  2. R2 · Stealing now. Default: each running steal shows the thief, the owner, the tier (or FORGE), where, MAP ›, the time left and a purple bar. These count down each second from BSP-Core's time, and a row goes when its end arrives. It comes from the live messages and the board read every 30 s.
  3. R3 · Flagged. Default: everyone with a FAST steal or a BURST in the window, most flags first. Each card shows their steals, failed and points taken, plus four boxes: fastest steal, BSP-Core's time for it, the shortest gap between two steals, and how long ago the last one was. A card opens their view as their board row does. "No FAST steals or bursts in 7 days" when none.
  4. R4 · The board. Default: the top 20 with ALL 23 › for the rest. Each row shows the rank, the name with best tier and a red (FAST) or gold (BURST) flag count, the points gained or lost in the window (blank when none), and the points. A computer adds totems held and steals as columns. "Board read 12 s ago from Frostwarf" heads the tab.
  5. R5 · The steals. Default: newest first, 50 at a time with MORE. Chips (All, Flagged, Completed, Failed, each with its count) and a name search narrow them. A steal that arrives while the page is open goes on top, lit for 10 seconds. The time reads "21:31" today, "Yest. 18:04", then "18 Nov 22:10", in the viewer's own time zone. A steal whose start was not seen reads "? of ?" with "start not seen (the server restarted)". An unclaimed totem reads "an unclaimed I".
  6. R6 · A player opened. Default: a tap on a board row, flagged card or name opens it under the row; tap again to close. It shows a summary line, then their points line over the window (steps, with red dots for FAST, gold for BURST, hollow for failed). The FAST warning (D627) shows only when they have a FAST steal. Then come their 5 newest steals, and ALL n STEALS (the list narrowed to them), NOTES (their staff notes) and PLAYER › (their page on the Players tab).
  7. R7 · MAP ›. Default: opens the Map tab with that server and world picked, centred on the block, with a pin there for 30 seconds. A map hidden by an Owner (D573), or none drawn, shows "No map of Stormfall's Overworld is shown on the panel." and stays on TOTEMS.
  8. R8 · Problems. Default: plain gold lines at the top, and red for the steals table: no server can read the board; a server only just started (the last board read stays shown, with its age); the points could not be saved; the steals table cannot be read; steals not verified (D628). The rest of the tab still shows.
  9. R9 · Words in game terms. Default: "took Wren_88's II" for a completed steal and "tried" for a failed one, FORGE for a Totem Forge's pour, and failure reasons in words ("Left the totem for too long"). Times read "0:19 of 0:40"; gaps read "1 min 41 s after their last" (a player's gender is never known, so never "her" or "his").
