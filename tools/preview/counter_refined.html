To confirm

  1. R1 the words' placeholders: {name} and {number}, up to 160 characters, starting "Welcome {name}, player number {number} on BSP!" (default); or %name% and %number%.
  2. R2 the welcome line on the other servers: with the server joined in grey first, "[BSP-Spawn] Welcome ..." (default, as drawn); or the same words everywhere with no server name.
  3. R3 the number in the welcome line after an adjustment: the number as shown on the board (the real list plus the adjustment, default); or the real count.
