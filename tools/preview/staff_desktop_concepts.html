Staff panel on a computer: mock-up set O

The same panel on a desktop or laptop browser at staff.buildsecureprotect.com. The phone design stays as picked (D273 to D279, D284 to D286); on a wide screen the page rearranges itself, nothing is a separate site. Three layouts for the main screen, each with Vexa opened. Then the refined login and staff accounts (set N, look A) as they look on a computer, with three points to confirm. Made-up names and numbers.

O-A. The phone page, widened

The five tabs move to a bar along the top with search and your name. The team cards sit in a column in the middle, and a player opens in a panel down the right instead of a sheet from the bottom. Closest to the phone: staff who use both find everything in the same order.

O-B. List and player side by side

A thin bar of icons on the left for the tabs. The players' list takes the left half; the right half is always the whole player page (stats, inventory, notes), the pulled-up sheet of R3 kept open. Click another name and the right half changes. Best for going through players one after another.

O-C. Dashboard

A labelled menu on the left. The servers as tiles along the top, the teams as cards in a grid of three, and a column on the right with the network chat as it happens and who of the staff is on the panel. A player opens as a card you can drag anywhere over the page (the tracking card from the map). The most on one screen; for a big monitor.

Set N refined: logging in and staff accounts on a computer (look A)

Look A as picked, with the list of backup codes and the gold lock-out line. On a phone these are the A frames of set N; on a computer the card stays the same size in the middle, and the staff page becomes a table.

Choices and points to confirm

  1. O1. The main screen on a computer. O-A: the phone page widened, tabs along the top and the player in a panel on the right. O-B: list and player side by side, the player page always open. O-C: a dashboard with server tiles, a grid of teams, live chat and staff on the right, and the player as a card you drag.
  2. O2. From what width the computer layout starts. From 900 pixels wide (tablets held sideways get it too). From 1200 pixels (laptops and up; tablets keep the phone page).
  3. R1 (set N). A wrong try on the login card shows a red line in the card, "Wrong password or code. 3 tries left, then this account locks for 15 minutes.", as in set N's C1; a lock shows when it opens again.
  4. R2 (set N). After FINISH on the setup page the page is replaced by the ten codes in a numbered list with COPY ALL and SAVE AS FILE; GO TO THE PANEL stays grey until "I have saved these somewhere safe" is ticked.
  5. R3 (set N). The staff page on a computer is a table: name and face, rank, email, status (logged in, locked until, link waiting), last login, and the ⋯ menu; the gold line above it. On a phone it stays the list of A3.
