Server alerts: three looks for the email and three for the panel

SU-167. The staff panel warns when a game server freezes (its port is open but it sends nothing) or crashes (its port is closed). Set BK is the email, set BL is how the panel shows it. Pick one per set; parts can be combined. Server names are the network's; the times, players and numbers are made up.

BK · the emailBL · the panelThe states every look uses

The states every look uses

CHECKING FROZEN CRASHED RESTARTING 1:40 / 5:00 NOT BACK SLOW BACK

Checking: silent for 20 s, the panel is making its three checks (no email yet). Frozen: three checks silent, its port still answers. Crashed: three checks silent, its port closed (crashed, or stopped without saying so). Restarting: it said it was stopping (a planned restart), counted against the grace time. Not back: a planned stop that ran past the grace time. Slow: still sending but very slow for a long time (only if question 6 turns it on). Back: sending again after an alert.

BK · the alert email

BK A · a plain letter

Plain text only: every mail app shows it the same, nothing to load. The subject says the server and what happened; the body says when it was last heard, what its port said, the three checks, what it was doing before, and what to do.

FromBSP staff panelToownerBSP-Frostwarf is frozen
BSP-Frostwarf is frozen: it stopped sending at 14:02:15
(Friday 27 November, UK time), 1 minute 20 seconds before
this email. Its port still answers, so the game is stuck
but the server process is still running.

Last heard     14:02:15, 11 players online, 48.2 ms a tick
Checked        14:02:55, 14:03:15, 14:03:35: silent each time
Its port       192.168.1.27:25565 answered at 14:03:35

A frozen server does not stop on its own: restart it in
Pterodactyl (Console, Restart; Kill if it will not stop).

WORLDS on the panel:
https://staff.buildsecureprotect.com/#worlds

The next alert email for BSP-Frostwarf can go at 15:03 at
the earliest (one an hour). You will get one more when it is
back.

BL · the alert on the panel

BL A · a banner on every tab, history under WORLDS

While an alert is open, a red strip sits at the top of every tab (blue for a planned restart in its grace time), so staff see it wherever they are. On WORLDS the tile says what happened in its own colour, and RECENT ALERTS under the grid lists the last ones with how long each lasted and whether an email went.
