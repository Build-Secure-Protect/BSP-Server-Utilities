## [0.7.0] - 2026-11-22

Offline players on the Players tab and the Map tab. Needs BSP-Server Utilities 0.18.0 on the game servers and one more database grant (`docs/STAFF_PANEL_SETUP.md`, Running it › Updating to panel 0.7.0).

### Added
- Offline players, the panel's server side: `POST /api/players/offline` (`q` any part of a name, `offset`, `limit` up to 50: everyone who ever joined and is not online now, the most recently seen first, then those with no record by name, with how many in all) and `POST /api/players/seen` (`player`: one player with whether they are online), for every rank. Each comes with where they were last seen (server, world, block, when, and whether it was a logout or the server's last 5-minute record) from the game servers' `bsp_su_seen` (BSP-Server Utilities 0.18.0), which needs one more grant: `GRANT SELECT ON bsp.bsp_su_seen TO 'bsp_panel'@'<host>';`. A place is shown only through a signed copy of that very row (the player, their name, the time, the server, world, block and kind), and an `actionKey` the panel cannot read shows no place at all; a row dated more than a day ahead never holds the top. Without the grant the players are still listed by name, and with game servers older than 0.18.0 the panel says to update them.
- An offline player's head is drawn from the skin last seen while the panel ran.
- Offline players on the Players tab: a grey OFFLINE group under the online groups, the most recently seen first, each with a faded head, the name in light grey, their tags, the server and world they logged off on and how long ago ("12 min", "3 h", "yest.", "4 d", "18 Oct"); "not recorded" last, by name. An Offline tile (a chip on a phone) and "· 1,284 offline" in the count; SHOW 50 MORE (20 at a time on a phone); the group folds and stays folded in this browser. The search box finds offline players by any part of the name too.
- An offline player's card: the same card as an online player's, greyed, with OFFLINE and when they were last seen; where they logged off with MAP ›, said when the server recorded no logout (its last 5-minute record), when nothing is recorded yet, when the record could not be checked, or when that world's map is not shown; Graves, Bank & Market, Message and Ban (no Kick or Teleport); messages, market, bounties, the items from their newest save and notes. A player who leaves while their card is open keeps it, now offline.
- The offline players are read again a few seconds after anyone leaves the network and every minute while shown; someone who logs on moves to their online group at once.
- `--demo` has 90 made-up offline players with places, one crash record and three never recorded.
- Offline players on the Map tab: "Show offline players" under the players window's search (in the list sheet on a phone), kept in this browser, adds a grey OFFLINE part under the servers: the most recently seen first with how long ago ("12m", "3h", "1d") and their world, MORE for the next ones, found by the window's search. Choosing one stops following, switches to the server and world where they logged off, centres on the block and puts their head there in grey, labelled "Name · logged off", until another player is chosen or their card is closed; their card in the corner (a small sheet on a phone) shows when and where, the hotbar from their last save, ITEMS, NOTES and OPEN CARD. A world whose map is not shown leaves the map where it was and says so; a player with no record gets the card without a pin; one who logs on is followed live.
- MAP › on an offline player's card opens the Map tab the same way.

### Changed
- More › Find a player opens an offline player's card on the Players tab (the separate offline page is gone), as do a name tapped in chat, the TOTEMS tab, the market, Bank & Market and Player messages.

## [0.6.0] - 2026-11-22

Private messages to players. Needs BSP-Server Utilities 0.17.0 on the game servers (players need its new modpack: protocol 10) and two more database grants (`docs/STAFF_PANEL_SETUP.md`, Running it › Updating to panel 0.6.0).

### Added
- Private messages to players, the page: a MESSAGES section in every player's card (the Players tab) and on an offline player's page (More › Find a player), under the keys. Staff lines on the right in violet with their writer, the player's on the left, the newest at the foot, a line arriving live lit for 10 seconds; earlier conversations of the last 90 days folded behind "Ended by …" (tap to show); a box of up to 256 characters with SEND (Enter), which keeps the words and says why when a line is refused; "Open since …, started by … · not seen yet" and END CONVERSATION, which asks "Sure? End" for 3 seconds. A MESSAGE key among the player's keys goes to the section, with a red dot while their reply waits.
- More › Player messages: the open conversations, replies waiting on staff first (lit red, with how long), then the rest ("sent 2 h", "offline · not seen it yet"); a tap opens the player at MESSAGES. A red count on More says how many replies wait on staff, kept live.
- `--demo` shows three made-up conversations, and SEND and END work in it.
- Private messages to players, the panel's server side: `POST /api/pmsg` (`id`, `name`: a player's conversations of the last 90 days, the open one with who started it, who is waiting and since when, and whether the player was told), `POST /api/pmsg/list` (the open conversations, those waiting on staff first, the longest wait first, with the count of waiting replies), `POST /api/pmsg/send` (`id`, `name`, `text`, up to 256 characters) and `POST /api/pmsg/end` (`id`, `name`), for every rank. Sends and ends go to the player's own game server, else to the busiest that takes them, and have their own allowance of 30 a minute for each staff member, as in game. An end is in the panel's log.
- The messages are read from the game servers' `bsp_su_talks` and `bsp_su_talk_lines`, which need two more grants: `GRANT SELECT ON bsp.bsp_su_talks TO 'bsp_panel'@'<host>';` and `GRANT SELECT ON bsp.bsp_su_talk_lines TO 'bsp_panel'@'<host>';`. A line is shown only through its signed copy, so nobody with the database alone can put words in anyone's mouth. New lines arrive live on every open page (a `pmsg` event with the waiting count).

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-21

The TOTEMS tab: who is stealing which totems and how fast. Needs BSP-Server Utilities 0.16.0 on the game servers and one more database grant (`docs/STAFF_PANEL_SETUP.md`, Running it › Updating to panel 0.5.0).

### Added
- The TOTEMS tab, between Map and Staff, for every rank:
  - "Stealing now", counting down each second.
  - The players flagged in the window (FAST or BURST), each with their fastest steal against BSP-Core's time, the shortest gap between two steals and their last steal.
  - BSP-Core's board with the points each player gained or lost in the window (the top 20, then ALL).
  - Every steal with where (MAP › opens the Map tab there, with a pin), when, from whom, the time taken against BSP-Core's time, and the gap since that player's last steal. Failed steals are shown dimmed, with BSP-Core's reason in words.
  - Windows of 24 hours, 7, 30 or 90 days, remembered on each device.
  - A player opens under their row: their points line (a red dot for a FAST steal, gold for a burst, hollow for a failed one), their newest steals, NOTES and PLAYER ›.
- `--demo` shows the tab with made-up steals and points, and a steal every two minutes.
- A steal is FAST when it took fewer server ticks than BSP-Core allows, so lag on a game server never flags an honest steal. A steal is shown only when its row matches its signed copy (the same steal, thief and end), so nobody with the database alone can put a steal under another player.
- The TOTEMS page's server side (the page itself follows): `POST /api/totems` (`days` 1, 7, 30 or 90) and `POST /api/totems/player` (`id`, `days`) for every rank. They give BSP-Core's network board with each player's points gained and lost in the window, the steals running now, every recorded steal (where, who from whom, tier, points, when, how long it took against what BSP-Core gave it, the time since that player's previous steal) and a line per thief. A steal that finished more than 2 s sooner than BSP-Core allows is `fast`, and three completed steals by one player within 10 minutes are a `burst`.
- The board is read from one game server every 30 s (BSP-Server Utilities 0.16.0's `totem_board`), and every change of a player's points is kept in the panel's own new `panel_points` table for 90 days (made by itself on start). Steals are read from the game servers' `bsp_su_steals`, which needs one more grant: `GRANT SELECT ON bsp.bsp_su_steals TO 'bsp_panel'@'<host>';`. Without it the page says so.
- Steals starting and ending anywhere reach open pages at once as `totems` events.
- The board is read from the same server while it answers, from the next able one when it cannot, and never from a server that has only just started. Nobody's points are set to 0 from an empty or cut board. A points save is all or none and never counted twice. Points gained and lost are summed by the database.
- Steals are shown only through each row's signed copy; rows without one are counted as "could not be verified". A steal whose end was missed leaves "stealing now" once its time has run out or a later board does not list it. Bursts and the time between steals also look at the day before the window. The page's answer is kept for 5 seconds, and made once for all open pages after a change. Database errors go to the panel's console, and the page says what to check in plain words.

## [0.4.2] - 2026-11-19

Which maps each server shows on the Map tab, chosen by an Owner. Only the panel changes: the game servers stay on BSP-Server Utilities 0.14.0, nothing in the modpack.

### Added
- `GET /api/maps` and `POST /api/maps/set` (Owners only): every map BlueMap has drawn, by server, shown or hidden; a change of one map or a whole server's (ALL, NONE) saved together, with the last change's who and when. Every change, refusal and failed save is in the panel's log.
- `/map/settings.json` leaves the hidden maps off its list, and a hidden map's files are refused, so a hidden map is not reachable by its address. Open pages get a `maps` event after a change. A map nobody chose is shown.
- The panel's database makes a `panel_maps` table by itself on start.
- `/map/` refuses an address with a `.` segment, so a hidden map cannot be reached that way either.
- Map tab, for Owners: a violet MAPS key on the bar opens a sheet over the map's right side (from the bottom on a phone) listing every server with a chip per map; a tap hides or shows that map at once, ALL and NONE do a whole server, the line under the list says what was saved with UNDO for that change, a failed save turns the line red and leaves the map as it was, and the foot names the last change. With every map hidden the Map tab says "No maps are shown.", with the MAPS key for Owners.

### Changed
- Map tab: an Owner's change reaches every open page within moments, without a reload. Anyone on a map that gets hidden moves to that server's Overworld (or the first map shown) with the line "That map was hidden by an Owner."; the Owner who hid it reads it in the sheet's saved line. A map shown again is on the keys at once.
- Map tab: choosing another server in the picker keeps the dimension when that server shows it (Nether to Nether), else its Overworld.
- Map tab: opens on the map you looked at last in this browser.
- Map tab: following a player into a dimension with no map shown says "which has no map shown".

## [0.2.1] - 2026-11-11

### Fixed
- Audit of the whole staff panel (2026-11-11, the repository's docs/TESTING.md):
  - The Redis listener took a refused SUBSCRIBE (a wrong password, a Redis user without the channels) as listening, and nothing about Redis was ever printed: every action then said "No answer". It now checks Redis's replies, says each change of state once in the console (`status` shows it any time), never stops for good, and refuses a message over 8 MB.
  - An action is sent only while the panel's Redis listener is subscribed and Redis took it (not through the table alone, which reached listening servers only every 30 s), and not to a game server whose snapshot says its answers cannot reach the panel: each refusal says why and nothing is sent.
  - Each action carries `until`, the moment the panel stops waiting; an UNDO taking items back, a market REFRESH and bans wait 15 s instead of 6.
  - An answer is taken only from the server asked; an action's own fields (an UNDO's, from a game server's answer) never replace who asked, their rank or the server; an UNDO is only ever an items change.
  - Item data typed into an add is taken only when the item pictures list it for that item (the picker's potions and books).
  - The live view goes to the pages at most every 5 s (D251; seven servers' snapshots redrew every page every second), with a named `ping` the page can watch.
  - A server is held by its own snapshot interval and dropped at once when it says it is stopping; a server whose snapshots are not signed with the panel's key is named in the console once an hour; the servers' table error is printed once, not every 5 s; the reason Cloudflare's token was refused is printed once a minute.
  - The database fails a request after 15 s instead of hanging every page; a database that cannot be used at start is said in one sentence.
  - Graves and items are asked of a server whose BSP-InventorySync can answer when the player's own cannot.
  - Chat: a stored row whose uid is not the signed line's is dropped; a signed line sent again live over 10 minutes late is not shown live; the table is read with an overlap so a line written out of order is not missed; invisible format characters become spaces, as in game.
  - Over https the login cookie is `__Host-bsp_panel`, which no other page of the domain can set.
  - `panel.properties` is written for a container: `listen.host=0.0.0.0`, `listen.port` empty for Pterodactyl's allocation, the database and Redis hosts asked for (localhost is the container itself); the action key's comment says all it can do.
  - A request that sends its headers slowly is closed after 15 s.
- The panel's own database (D425): accounts, logins, notes and the staff room in `database.panelName` (`bsp_panel`) with a user only the panel has; the shared tables are read through `database.name`. Anyone with the game servers' database password could make themselves Owner before. The guide gives the SQL.
- The panel's own signing key (D426): `signingKey` (Ed25519, only in `panel.properties`) signs every action and the notes counts; game servers check them with `panelKey` (public). Both are written on the first start (or added to an older file). The console's `action-key` prints `panelKey` only.
- Only the Owner lifts a ban on staff (D427); on More › Bans an Admin sees UNBAN and EDIT on a staff member's ban dimmed with "Owner".
- A Moderator with the items power logs in for 48 hours, as Admins do; giving or taking the power ends their open logins (D428).
- Note cards get their author's rank (D320).
- The pages after the audit: nothing redraws under a field being typed in or a chooser being used (the Players tab, the sheet, Teleport, Find a player, the tag picker, chat search, the item picker, the map card); the ban form's BAN works after the reason is typed; chat adds new lines without rebuilding, and scrolls back past 1,500 lines; a watchdog says "Not live" after 45 s without a word from the panel; after a reconnect the notes, chat and staff room are read again; every request gives up after 20 s and says what went wrong in plain words (never a raw error page); sends cannot be done twice; 16 px fields and a ± key for coordinates on phones; the staff ⋯ menu is not cut off on a phone; the gold line no longer covers the "Not live" banner; cosmetic Curios slots are shown; a SURE? keeps the keyboard's focus.
- The setup guides, read step by step against the code: Redis follows BSP-Core's settings; the `[webPanel]` lines edited in place (a second section stops a server); addresses as seen from a container; the allocation on 127.0.0.1 with cloudflared as a service; a Java egg to import; the jar under one name; the setup link opened again after Cloudflare's login; utf8mb4; the full BSP-Core jar; serverIds the same as BSP-InventorySync's and Velocity's; a "Running it" part (the off switch, a leaked key, backups, updates, restarts); BlueMap's resource packs kept by `rsync`.
