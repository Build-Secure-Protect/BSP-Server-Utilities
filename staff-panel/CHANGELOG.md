# BSP staff web panel: changelog

## [Unreleased]

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-09

### Added
- The panel program (0.1.0, not released): Cloudflare Access checked on every request; staff accounts with three ranks; the first Owner from the console; one-time setup links (15 minutes) with a password, an authenticator QR code and ten backup codes; logins of 7 days (Moderators) or 48 hours (Admins, Owners); five wrong tries lock for 15 minutes; Owner resets; the live feed of the game servers' players from Redis, or the database when Redis is down; the login, setup and More pages; the phone layout with tabs at the bottom and, from 900 pixels wide, the menu on the left with staff online; `--demo` for a look on one computer.
- The Players tab: on a phone the servers as chips, the players grouped by team (or by server) in cards that fold, each with their head, AFK, staff and game-mode marks, where they are, ten hearts and their armour and hunger (LOW at 3 hearts or less), and a sheet from the bottom with the stats as the game's icons, XP, air under water and potion effects; on a computer the server tiles, the teams in one to three columns by width, compact rows and the player as a card to drag that keeps its place. A search box. Players' heads cut from their skins, fetched from Mojang by the panel and served from its own address.
- Actions: Kick in a player's sheet or card (every rank; a reason, then a second tap within 3 seconds) and the Broadcast page under More (Admins and Owners: chat line or title with `|` for the subtitle, colour, one player or everyone, one server or all, `&` codes; the line or title drawn as the game shows it while typing; SEND with a second tap). Signed with `actionKey` (made on the first start, added to an older `panel.properties`; the console's `action-key` prints it for the game servers), sent through Redis and the notices table, the game server's own answer shown. Every action in the panel's log.
- Graves (D295 to D302): a Graves page under More (any player who ever joined, by name, online or not) and a Graves key in a player's sheet or card; each grave from every server, newest first, marked in the world, not placed or put away, with its place, age, XP and its stacks as slots; RETURN (Admins and Owners, a second tap) on graves that may be returned, sent to the server the player is on (once more to the one named if they moved); the items once BSP-InventorySync can list them (SU-IS3).
- A second audit the same day: snapshots must name the server they came from and be under a minute old; graves are asked of a server whose BSP-InventorySync can answer; a return is sent on only to a server sending to the panel; a page replaced by a newer one stops and offers to reconnect (no loop); pages still closing count against the limits; the Graves page never draws over another tab; heads fetched within 10 s in all, any case of hex, no other port; the panel's tables in utf8mb4.
- Hardened in the audit of 2026-11-09: one change at a time per account, live pages that stop reading dropped, action limits, signed snapshots and answers only, head fetch caching, the reconnect banner, and more (the repository's docs/TESTING.md).
