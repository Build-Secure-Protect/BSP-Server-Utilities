## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added
- Hardened in the audit of phase 5 (2026-11-11): a game server that does not answer is asked once, not by every page in turn (failures remembered 8 s; REFRESH at most once in 5 s); a page never stays on "Counting" after missing a draw's end; a server stuck saying a draw runs never blocks DRAW; the winner card counts the players who were in; the end of a draw always tells the pages once; the live feed never stops for the lottery watch.
- The whole setup, step by step (D333): `docs/STAFF_PANEL_SETUP.md` (also in the vault's User Notes): what to install where and in what order (BSP-Velocity Additions 0.2.0 on the proxy; BSP-Server Utilities, BSP-Core 1.16+, BSP-InventorySync 0.7.0 and BSP-Teleport API 2 on the game servers), the game servers' settings and the action key, the panel's Pterodactyl server with `panel.properties` line by line, Cloudflare Access (Zero Trust Free, One-time PIN, the staff policy, the application, the team domain and AUD tag) before the tunnel (Networking › Tunnels, cloudflared on the node, the published application route), the first Owner, the staff and their ranks, the item pictures and the map, a check list and what to do when something does not work. The README points to it.
- More › Market and More › Lottery (SU-52; D400 to D415, look A of set Y): the Market Board and the Wanted Board on one page under a switch, with the count of each; a lot as a card on a phone (seller with their head and a teal dot when online, the price in pieces, its stacks as slots with their pictures, listed and ends, the server it was listed on) and as one wide row on a computer; a tapped slot shows the item as in the game (a shulker box with what it holds); orders with the item, how many still to go with a bar, the price each and in all, the buyer; a search over item and player names, four sorts, "market read 40 s ago" and REFRESH; BSP-Core's database down said as such, never an empty board. "On the market: 1 lot, 2 orders" on a player's sheet and their Find a player page opens the market filtered to them (a chip with ✕); a seller's or buyer's name opens their sheet, or their Find a player page when offline. The Lottery page: the pot in large gold figures with DRAW beside it (SURE? within 3 s; dimmed with "Admin" for Moderators; dimmed with the reason when the pot is empty or nobody is in), the names in (search, online in teal), the last 10 wins; on a computer in two columns. A draw anywhere counts 5 to 1 in the pot card's corner on every open panel, then the card shows the winner and the pieces for a minute, and a gold line at the top of every page says who drew and who won until it is closed.
- The market and the lottery, the panel's side (SU-51; D400 to D415): `POST /api/market` (every rank; asked of a live server with BSP-Core 1.16, one whose database is connected first, then the busiest; kept 30 s for every page; `refresh` asks again at once with BSP-Core reading its database first; BSP-Core's database down answered as such, never as an empty market), `POST /api/lottery` (every rank; kept 3 s; with the network's draw when one is counting), `POST /api/lottery/draw` (Admins and Owners; 403 for Moderators; on the busiest server whose database is connected; in the panel's log). The live feed carries each server's `market` and, while a draw counts anywhere, `lottery` (phase, since, server); a draw beginning and its end (the winner and pieces, or that it ended without one) go to every open page as `lottery` events, with who drew when it was drawn from the panel. `--demo` has a made-up market, lottery and a draw that counts down.
- Hardened in the audit of phase 4 (2026-11-11): the map's card keeps what is being typed and its scroll through live updates; following survives BlueMap replacing a marker; map switches can never hang; pins and their box as intended; BSP-Teleport asked at most every 30 s for the pins; only BlueMap's own page and scripts run at `/map/`; the setup's config copy replaces instead of nesting (the repository's docs/TESTING.md).
- The map's setup (SU-49; D379 to D385, D388, D397, D398): `docs/STAFF_MAP_SETUP.md` (BlueMap 5.29's own Pterodactyl server on Java 25, the read-only mounts, the panel's `map.root`, the first run as the trial, what to do afterwards) and `tools/gen_bluemap.py`, which writes BlueMap's whole configuration: a flat map of every server's overworld, Nether and End and of other modded dimensions in use (never the storage grids), everything generated drawn, BlueMap's own web server off. Map names are `<server>__<dimension>` with only letters, digits and `_` (BlueMap turns anything else in a map's name into `_`).
- The Map tab on a phone (SU-48; D393, D395 R4): the map fills the screen between the top and the tabs, with the server and its dimensions above it; a players button opens everyone online as a sheet (a tap follows them); the followed player is a sheet from the bottom, small at first (where they are, stats, Items, Notes, Stop following), a tap on its bar pulls it up for everything (hotbar or items, Teleport, Graves, Kick, Ban, notes).
- The Map tab on a computer (SU-47; D389 to D395): everyone online by server on the left (a search; the one you follow in gold), BlueMap's map in the middle with the panel's own bar over it (the server, its dimensions, the pins to show, "Following …" with ✕ and "centre" after you move the map yourself); a tap on a name or a head follows that player, across servers and dimensions (the map goes with them and a gold line says so), with a dotted gold trail of their last 2 minutes; their card over the map, to drag: where they are ("underground" below y 50), stats, effects, the hotbar, ITEMS for everything (as on the Players tab), NOTES, STOP FOLLOWING, TELEPORT, GRAVES, KICK, BAN. A tapped warp or spawn pin opens a box with "Move <player> here" for the player followed. A followed player who leaves shows greyed where they were last with the time, and is followed again if back within 10 minutes. The panel's pages may frame only the panel itself.
- The map's live data (SU-46; D259, D386): BlueMap's page at `/map/` gets everyone online from the panel's own feed, each on the map of their server and dimension (map ids `<server>_<dimension>`, docs/API.md), with their heads from their skins, moving every few seconds; warps and spawns as pins (portal warps in a set hidden at first), labels made safe; BlueMap's live stream refused so its page keeps asking. Graves as pins wait for BSP-InventorySync to list every grave (request SU-IS4).
- The map's files (SU-45; D259, D381, D382, D397): with `map.root` in `panel.properties` set to BlueMap's web folder (mounted read-only from BlueMap's own server), the panel serves BlueMap's page and map at `/map/`, behind the panel's login: BlueMap's `.gz` files sent as gzip, tiles not drawn yet answered with nothing (204), unchanged files not sent again; looser page rules on `/map/` only (BlueMap's page needs them), framed only by the panel itself; no path out of the folder, links leading outside refused. A login is believed for 30 seconds on map files (forgotten at logout), so a view's hundreds of tiles do not each read the database.
- Chat (D304 to D313): every game server's public chat, live, with each server's name and colour, a divider for each day, the player's name to tap for their sheet; lines naming you or asking for staff ("staff", "admin", "mod", "help") marked red and counted on the Chat tab's badge (a dot for other new lines); older lines when scrolled to the top, back to 30 days; search by words, player, server and day, a result shown among the lines around it. Speaking as "[Staff] <name>" (every rank) or "[Server]" (Admins and Owners), to one server or the network: one signed action per server, the line shown at once with "✓ N servers", and a server that did not answer named with RETRY. 256 characters of plain text. Lines arrive through Redis and are read from `<prefix>web_chat` every 3 seconds for anything Redis missed; lines older than 30 days are dropped every hour. On a phone the Chat tab; on a computer the column on the right (over 1300 px always open, 900 to 1300 px behind the CHAT button). Every line said is in the panel's log.
- The Staff tab (D314 to D319, D327 to D329): one live staff room for everyone on the panel, with each person's rank and a divider for each day, "@Name" lit when it names you; your own line can be deleted for 5 minutes (a long press or right click; "deleted" stays in its place), an Owner deletes any. Messages to one staff member or to all staff, waiting until they next open the panel: unread ones with a teal edge, GOT IT, REPLY under the message (which marks it read), + NEW MESSAGE (up to 1000 characters); your own show "Read 21:40" or "Read by 4 of 6" with the names on a tap; your own deleted within 5 minutes, an Owner's any time. The Staff tab's badge counts unread messages and room lines naming you (a dot for other new lines). On a phone ROOM and MESSAGES under one switch; on a computer side by side. Kept 30 days; a message somebody has not read is kept until they have (or their account is removed). New tables `<prefix>panel_room`, `<prefix>panel_messages`, `<prefix>panel_reads`. Deletions are in the panel's log. Nothing of it reaches the game.
- A player's items (SU-39; D256, D279, D336 to D338, D360, D372): in the sheet or card, laid out as the game's inventory screen (armour on the left, the player's head, Curios and the offhand on the right, the 27 slots and the hotbar), the ender chest, Curios beyond three, backpacks and danks folded below with where each sits ("on Curios Back", "inside Shulker Box"), "removals only while offline" or "read only (linked)" where BSP-InventorySync says so; a locked Shatter Totem marked. Live, with its age, and read again every 15 seconds while the sheet is open (and on ↻); an offline player's items from their last save, with its age and server. A tapped slot opens a game-style tooltip (name, enchantments, durability, lore, a shulker box's contents, the item's id and slot). Until the picture export exists, items are grey tiles with two letters and names made from their ids. More › Find a player: anyone who ever joined by the start of their name, online ones first; an online player opens on the Players tab, an offline one on the page with their items, Graves and notes. Reads have their own allowance, 30 a minute per staff member, apart from the 10 actions.
- Adding and removing items (SU-40; D257, D335, D339, D342, D343, D361, D363, D371): + ADD on the inventory and on the ender chest, backpacks and danks that take items (an item id such as `create:brass_ingot` or just `diamond`, and 1, 16, 32, 64 or a typed count up to 6400), REMOVE in a slot's tooltip (1, half or ALL), each with a second tap (SURE?). The game server's answer shows under INVENTORY with UNDO and the minutes left (5 minutes; the same staff member only; an UNDO cannot put back a removed backpack, and a re-added item may land in another slot); the changed slots are outlined gold until the next read; an offline player's view says changes reach them at their next login. Admins and Owners always; Moderators when an Owner ticks "May add and remove items" in their ⋯ menu on the staff page (a gold ITEMS mark beside their rank; new column `items_power` in `<prefix>panel_staff`, added to an older table at start). Others see the keys dimmed with "Admin or ITEMS". Every change and every power given or taken is in the panel's log. Sent to the player's server, once more to the server named if they moved.
- Item pictures and the picker (SU-41; D271, D340, D341, D360, D373): the newest export from an admin's `/su icons`, read from the shared database every minute, checked whole (SHA-256), unpacked in memory (only its two lists and its pictures, size-limited). Items show as their game pictures with their English names and enchantment names (two-letter tiles and names made from ids when an item is not in it, or before any export); + ADD opens the creative picker: the tabs along the top, a search by name or `@mod`, slots, the chosen item with its count, ADD with a second tap; potions and other variants keep their data. More › Items: the export in use (items, tabs, who made it, when, mods, size), a row of pictures, how to make a fresh one, the last five exports. The browser keeps the pictures (a day for the list, a week for the sheets, a new address for each export). `--demo` has made-up pictures.
- A grave's items on the Graves page (SU-42; D298, D301, D302): each grave's stacks as slots like a chest with their pictures, a tap for the name, enchantments, durability and what a shulker box holds; backpacks and danks inside the grave below it with the slot they are in. Read as the page opens (every rank); the counts with a reason when they cannot be listed.
- Teleports (SU-43; D258, D344 to D349, D364 to D366, D374): TELEPORT in a player's sheet or card opens SPAWN (each server's `/setspawn` point), WARP (by server, restricted ones marked, portal warps folded at the end), PLAYER (everyone online by server, with a search) and COORDS (server, world, x, y, z filled with where they stand; "on the ground" on their own server only; the middle of the block). One tap moves them; the answer below. GO TO THEM and BRING THEM TO ME at the top when your own account is in game. TELEPORT greyed with "arriving on <server>" while they arrive, or where BSP-Teleport does not answer. Every rank; each move in the panel's log. The warps and spawns are read from a game server and kept 30 seconds.
- Network bans (SU-44; D255, D350 to D359, D367 to D370, D375, D376): BAN in a player's sheet or card for Admins and Owners (dimmed with "Admin" for Moderators; with "Owner" on a staff member for an Admin): the screen the player will get, previewed as you type; 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, custom days and hours, or permanent; the reason they read (160 characters) and a note only staff read; their earlier bans in gold. BAN, then SURE?, then LAST CHECK with their name typed (checked again by the panel). Staff (a panel account or a staff role in game) are banned by the Owner alone; an Owner by nobody. More › Bans: ACTIVE and PAST, a search by name, + BAN for any name who joined, UNBAN with SURE?, EDIT (keep the end or a new length, the reason, the note); past bans kept for good with who lifted them. Every ban, change and lift in the panel's log. `--demo` has made-up bans.
- Hardened in the audit of phase 3 (2026-11-10): a ban on staff is changed by the Owner alone, and the ban form says before the two checks when a player is staff; an UNDO is spent at once (never twice) and kept when the game server changed nothing; a grave's items read one grave at a time; a broken picture export read once, on its own thread; a rank change ends the items power (the repository's docs/TESTING.md).
- Player notes and tags (D266, D267, D320 to D326, D330 to D332): tags beside names on the Players list, the sheet and card, chat lines, search results and the Graves page (two, then "+1"; all in the sheet), + TAG in the sheet opening a picker (tick from the list, or type a new one, which joins the list grey until an admin colours it); any staff member puts tags on and takes them off. Notes as a section of the player's sheet, seen by every staff member with author and time; the author or an Admin edits ("edited", the earlier words kept in the panel's log) or deletes (a second tap); plain text up to 1000 characters. More › Tags: the tags typed by staff waiting for a colour, then the list with how many players carry each; Admins and Owners colour, rename and remove (a second tap). Every note and tag change is in the panel's log. Each online player's notes count goes to the game servers (signed `web_notes`, about every 30 seconds and at once on a change) for the hover in game. New tables `<prefix>panel_notes`, `<prefix>panel_tags`, `<prefix>panel_player_tags`.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-09

### Added
- The panel program (0.1.0, not released): Cloudflare Access checked on every request; staff accounts with three ranks; the first Owner from the console; one-time setup links (15 minutes) with a password, an authenticator QR code and ten backup codes; logins of 7 days (Moderators) or 48 hours (Admins, Owners); five wrong tries lock for 15 minutes; Owner resets; the live feed of the game servers' players from Redis, or the database when Redis is down; the login, setup and More pages; the phone layout with tabs at the bottom and, from 900 pixels wide, the menu on the left with staff online; `--demo` for a look on one computer.
- The Players tab: on a phone the servers as chips, the players grouped by team (or by server) in cards that fold, each with their head, AFK, staff and game-mode marks, where they are, ten hearts and their armour and hunger (LOW at 3 hearts or less), and a sheet from the bottom with the stats as the game's icons, XP, air under water and potion effects; on a computer the server tiles, the teams in one to three columns by width, compact rows and the player as a card to drag that keeps its place. A search box. Players' heads cut from their skins, fetched from Mojang by the panel and served from its own address.
- Actions: Kick in a player's sheet or card (every rank; a reason, then a second tap within 3 seconds) and the Broadcast page under More (Admins and Owners: chat line or title with `|` for the subtitle, colour, one player or everyone, one server or all, `&` codes; the line or title drawn as the game shows it while typing; SEND with a second tap). Signed with `actionKey` (made on the first start, added to an older `panel.properties`; the console's `action-key` prints it for the game servers), sent through Redis and the notices table, the game server's own answer shown. Every action in the panel's log.
- Graves (D295 to D302): a Graves page under More (any player who ever joined, by name, online or not) and a Graves key in a player's sheet or card; each grave from every server, newest first, marked in the world, not placed or put away, with its place, age, XP and its stacks as slots; RETURN (Admins and Owners, a second tap) on graves that may be returned, sent to the server the player is on (once more to the one named if they moved); the items once BSP-InventorySync can list them (SU-IS3).
- A second audit the same day: snapshots must name the server they came from and be under a minute old; graves are asked of a server whose BSP-InventorySync can answer; a return is sent on only to a server sending to the panel; a page replaced by a newer one stops and offers to reconnect (no loop); pages still closing count against the limits; the Graves page never draws over another tab; heads fetched within 10 s in all, any case of hex, no other port; the panel's tables in utf8mb4.
- Hardened in the audit of 2026-11-09: one change at a time per account, live pages that stop reading dropped, action limits, signed snapshots and answers only, head fetch caching, the reconnect banner, and more (the repository's docs/TESTING.md).
